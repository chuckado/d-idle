d.idle redesign, round two

The first attempt rearranged cards on a scrolling grid. You said it still has dead space, does not fit one screen, and the inventory is a mess. This round starts from a height budget: both screens are frames that fill the window, and every picture below is a render at an exact window size.

Fits one screenNo scrolling at 1920 x 1080, 1440 x 900, 1366 x 768 and at browser-window sizes down to 1280 x 620. The phone is a designed arrangement, not an overflow.
Zero dead spaceTiles tile the frame exactly. A region with nothing to say gives its room to its neighbour.
One character canvasHero and mercenary in one frame, storage as one connected area, a real tooltip, a right-click menu.

Watch: before and after

  • The picture is sized by the window's height, so it is always as large as it can be and never cut.
  • One rail to its right holds what you act on: Needs you, Orders, and the map you send the hero with.
  • One strip under it holds what you glance at: Progress, Journey, Health.
  • Hero stats, Why, Tally, Thoughts, Gear condition, the logs and the onboarding card are one click away, not tiles.

After your screenshot: the zone menu no longer covers the map

  • It was the menu, not the layout: the zone menu opened as a sheet over the bottom rows of the map. It now never sits on a zone button.
  • The area map was cropped to fill its tile. It is now scaled to fit, whole.
  • Browser-window sizes are part of the check now: 1914 x 930, 1904 x 1000, 1536 x 730 and 1280 x 620, next to the full-screen sizes. On short windows the zone rows drop to one line and the strip gets shorter, so all zones always show.

Try it at an exact window size

Fake data, placeholder shapes. Click a zone on the map, press Repair, cancel an order; the small "demo" button at the bottom left switches scenarios (quiet, a queue with a failed order, a blocked journey).

Character: before and after

  • One cell size for the dolls, the pack, the stash and the belt, taken from the window's height.
  • Hero and mercenary side by side, gear sets as a bar under them, both stat sheets under that.
  • Storage is one block: the pack over the stash, with the belt, the selected item's buttons and the recent item actions beside the stash.
  • A real tooltip beside the cell: requirements for you and for the mercenary, and the comparison with what is worn. Right-click for every action. Gold, repair and sell are chips in the bar.

Try it

Hover an item for the tooltip, right-click for the menu, click an item and then a doll slot to equip. Light Plate is selected: the armor slot is red because the hero lacks the level and strength; the mercenary's is green.
