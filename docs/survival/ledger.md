# Per-zone ledger and the shadow controller (survival slice S-C)

> status: current (card survC). Implements design-2026-11-14.md sections 5.3 to 5.6, 8 and 10 for slice S-C: log and API only, NO behaviour change. Tags: V verified (how), S secondary, I inferred, U unverified. Nothing here ran against a live game or a recorded real log.

## What shipped

- `internal/survival` (data in `params.json`, every constant tagged U): `Ledger` (per (area, difficulty) rows with exponential decay over field time, half-life 20 min; pooled `k_hero`), `KZone`, `KHero`, hard events (chicken x1.5, merc death x1.5, death x2.5, relaxing with time away), the power-change rule (a row learned at another power, more than 10 percent off, is dropped), `ForcedOut` (the dial's "observed cost that forces a step down"), `MarkLeft` / `Locked` (a zone left for cost stays closed until the hero's power moves by more than 10 percent), `Choose` (design 5.3: candidates, eligibility with the enter line for a new zone and the stay line for the current one, score `xpPerMin + lambda x netGoldPerMin` amortised over the stay, switching margin 15 percent, depleted zone -> next safe one or a fresh game, empty set -> first field plus "needs you"), `Revive` (design 5.5), `Economy` (xp and net gold per minute), `GlanceOf` (words only).
- Persistence: its own versioned file, `ledger.json` in the instance directory next to `deaths.json` (`paths.Instance.Ledger`, `store.File`, version 1). Additive: a bot without this package never reads it; a missing file is an empty ledger; unknown fields load; a file from a newer build is refused and never overwritten (the wire then starts empty and does not save). The brief said "in the state file": a separate file keeps the old bot's state files untouched, which is the same promise.
- Bot hooks (passive): `Deps.ZoneChoice` (called after the `zone shadow:` line, logs `zone choice:` and, with a dead merc, `zone revive:`), `Deps.ZoneObserve` (area summary, chicken, death, merc alive-to-dead edge) and the fighter's `OnSummary` chain (`survFight`) for the per-fight damage that `k_zone` and `k_hero` compare with the model. Wiring in `cmd/d2bot/survivalwire.go`, both backends.
- API: `GET /api/survival` (`internal/watch/survivalapi.go`, `Hub.SetSurvivalSource`): `{"enabled":false}` until the bot reports a choice, then `{"enabled":true,"survival":{schema,zone{id,name,margin,dial,why{code,text}},alternatives[],merc?,fresh?,needsYou?}}`. WORDS ONLY (user answer 2): `margin` is `safe`, `risky` or `too hard`; a test walks the JSON and fails on any number outside an `id` and on a digit in any `text`. Codes are the closed list in `internal/survival/why.go`.

## Deliberate limits (shadow)

- Candidates are the plan's steps of the difficulty (no quest or waypoint reachability, no navigation faults): I.
- Depleted = the planner's `cleared` mark. The fresh-game rule: the best eligible zone is depleted and the next safe zone is worth less than `freshRatio` (0.5, U) of it, or none is safe: a fresh game on the same zone. The user's answer allows fresh games as a zone tool; "the next is above the line" is read as "the next safe zone".
- Revive quote is the constant 400 (U; swap7 paid 360). Potion price 50, travel 2 min, gold per kill from the plain-kill table of internal/data: all U.
- Taken per kill: `FightSummary.Damage` is the sum of life drops between samples (healing not netted out; V in dodge.go), predicted taken per kill is the margin model's typical pack. The ratio is therefore biased up whenever potions heal in a fight (U); `k_zone` is clamped to 0.5 to 8.
- The hero power scalar for the 10 percent rule is max life, times 1.25 with a living merc (I, U). It ignores the belt on purpose.

## Observed intake (card intake)

Why: the watch screen showed a hero that "does not take damage" and nothing recorded how much it takes. The model's `Taken` is fight-only and predicted; this is what the poll actually saw. Log and API only, no behaviour change, no knob (always on).

- Source: `internal/bot/hpfields.go` `noteHP`, called on every alive poll of the observer (V by unit test, not live). `lifeLost` is the sum of life decreases between consecutive polls; gains (potions, regeneration) are ignored. A poll where `MaxLife` changed (level-up, gear swap, aura) is only rebased and counts as 0, so a max-life change is never damage. A death sample (life 0) counts as the drop it is. The older `dmgTaken` running counter is unchanged (it does count a max-life drop).
- `minute` line, additive keys: `lifeLost` (this minute), `hitsTaken` (polls that saw a drop), `hpPolls` (samples in the minute). `area summary` carries `lifeLost` of the stay. `d2dsoakstat.Minute` reads them (`HasIntake` false for old logs); `levelsim -shadow` sums them per stay and prints `intake N/min` in the note column when present.
- Under-count (S, by construction): the poll rate is the loop tick, so two hits landing between two polls give one drop, and a heal landing in the same interval as a hit nets out. `lifeLost` is a lower bound and `hitsTaken` a lower bound of the hits; `hpPolls` per minute tells how coarse the sample was (a slow tick under-counts more). Potion heals between hits are not netted, they are ignored, so the sum is not "damage minus healing".
- `/api/glance` field `intake` `{lifeLostPerMin, window}`: mean `lifeLost` over the last `window` (up to 5) completed minute lines, one decimal; absent until the first minute line or without a source. It is a number for developers; the cockpit maps it to light, moderate or heavy against max life later (thresholds not decided here). Town minutes dilute it.
- Ledger: `ZoneStats.intakeLife` and `intakeSec` (decayed together with the same half-life as the fight sums; `omitempty`, ledger version stays 1, an old file reads 0). Fed by `EvIntake` from the area summary of each stay; `Ledger.IntakePerMin(zone)` gives life lost per minute and the seconds it rests on. It is separate from `Taken`/`k_zone`, which stay the model-vs-fight comparison; the danger/power margin can use it as the observed intake.
- Potion versus regeneration split: not done (needs the heal source per poll; `potionsUsed` per minute is already on the line).
- Tests: `go test -p 1 ./internal/bot -run Intake`, `./internal/survival -run LedgerIntake`, `./internal/watch -run GlanceIntake`, `./tools/levelsim -run Intake` (series with decreases, heals, level-up and max-life drop, potion; Paladin and Sorceress). Not run live.

## Realized yield, mana floor, fresh-game guard, revive loop (card survfix2)

Source: [A/B screen analysis](ab-screen-analysis-2026-11-16.md) section 6, proposals 1 to 5. Everything below acts only with `bot.zoneModel` 1 (`survival.Input.Active`); the shadow (zoneModel 0) keeps its old numbers. Tags: V = unit test (named), U = not run in a game.

- **Realized yield (V, `TestRealizedYieldOverridesPrior`, Paladin and Sorceress).** `EvXP` with `Seconds` (the area summary) feeds `ZoneStats.yieldXP/yieldSec` (decayed together by `halfLifeMinutes`; `omitempty`, ledger version stays 1). `Ledger.BlendYield(zone, prior, archetype)`: no weight below `yieldMinMinutes` (3, the trust floor: a bad minute never moves the value), then weight `m/(m+yieldShrinkMinutes)` capped at `yieldMaxWeight` (0.9), so the model prior fades as minutes accumulate and never vanishes. The `zone choice:` line prints `xp/min` of the value and `realized N (model M)` when the blend is live. Why: the screen predicted 152 xp/min for Burial Grounds and the hero realized 34 (density is not in the Economy).
- **Mana floor (V, `TestStarvedZoneLosesValue`).** The fighter counts watchdog lines "cannot cast" where an attack was ready but dearer than the mana pool (`FightSummary.Starved`, one per logged line, at most one per 5 s); the ledger keeps a decayed `starved` count per zone. At `starveCasts` (per archetype) the zone's value is multiplied by `starveValueMul` (0.5) and the line says `mana-starved`. The first sample is the 26 watchdog events of seed 101 (U: the constants are guesses; the replay does not carry starvation).
- **Per class and build data.** `params.json` `archetypes` overrides `yieldShrinkMinutes` and `starveCasts` by power profile id (`caster`, `bruiser`, ...); no class or skill name in Go.
- **Fresh-game guard (V, `TestFreshGameNeedsKnownOffenceAndMerc`).** With the model deciding, `fresh_game` is refused while the offence was not read (`src=default`) or the merc is dead; the line ends `fresh game refused: offence unread|merc dead` and the next safe zone (or the cleared best one) is taken instead.
- **Revive logging and loop breaker (V, `TestReviveLoopTripsAndResets`, `TestZoneMercReviveLoopBreaker`).** Always logged (also zoneModel 0): `merc revive: decision` (revive, zone, gain, cost, why; model only), `merc revive: alive again`, `merc revive: he died` with `afterSec` and `quick`, and the `zone revive:` shadow line now carries the zone, gain and cost. Breaker (zoneModel 1 only): `bot.mercReviveLoopN` (3) quick deaths (`bot.mercReviveLoopMin`, 10 minutes after a revive) in one zone close it to reviving for an hour (U: fixed to one hour, equal to `noReviveMinutes`); a death after a longer life resets the count; N 0 is off.
- Not done: calibrating the constants on a rescreen; the starvation count does not yet raise the mana margin of the zone, only its value.
