## 2. Root cause 1: the controller never learned in lockstep (V)
Evidence: in all 91 `zone choice:` lines of the four margin logs read, every alternative prints `k 1.00`, also after chickens, merc deaths and a death (Paladin 101 chickened at minute 107 and 110, 103 lost the merc 5 times in Dark Wood and Stony Field, both with `k 1.00` on the next decision). The ledger cannot be that blind by design (`chickenMul` 1.5, `mercDeathMul` 1.5, `deathMul` 2.5, `either` rows, `NoRevive` after two merc deaths).
Cause (V, code): `cmd/d2bot/survivalwire.go` stamped hard events with `ev.At`, which is the bot clock (`Env.Clock.Now`), but judged them with `time.Now()`. Under lockstep the bot clock is the `Pacer`, which counts from the Unix epoch (`internal/d2dinput/pacer.go`: zero `Base` is `time.Unix(0, 0)`), so every chicken, death and merc death was 56 years old when the controller looked: `hardNow` decayed to 1, `count` (windows) saw nothing, `ForcedOut` never fired, `NoRevive` never armed. Fight and intake events (stamped `time.Now()`) were the only live memory. On a wall clock (the live bot) both clocks agree, so the defect shows only in lockstep; the screen therefore tested a controller with no hard-event memory.
Fix (done, small): `newSurvivalShadow` takes the clock (`Env.Clock.Now`; `be.Clock.Now` in `d2dbackend.go`, `e.Clock.Now` in `live.go`; nil = wall) and uses it for `Choose`, the revive decision, area events without a stamp and fight events. Test `TestLedgerNeedsOneClock` states the contract (same clock: a death a minute ago forces the zone out; wall now against epoch stamps: it reads ancient).
Consequence for the three failures: I expect the repeated chickens (101: chicken, death, chicken within 5 minutes), the merc loop (103) and the Sorceress's repeated bad zones to be damped by the ledger once it works; this is I until a rescreen measures it. The first chicken after a fresh start is not prevented by it (the ledger learns after the event).

## 5. Sorceress XP deficit (V from `abreport` and the three margin logs)
- The margin arm sent the hero to **Burial Grounds / Crypt** (areas 17 and 18) for 27+15 minutes (legacy 4): predicted 152 xp/min (143 for Mausoleum), realized **34 xp/min** over the 41 minutes (3, 5, 69 in Burial Grounds; 107, 48 in Crypt), against 188 to 313 xp/min realized in Stony Field. The same prediction (152) appears for Burial Grounds, Crypt and Mausoleum because the Economy is `XPPerKill x gap x 60/(perKill + moveSecondsPerKill)` with perKill 2.3 s (`dps=11.5 src=skill`): it models density as unlimited.
- Why the hero did not get there (V, log): `combat: watchdog cannot cast (no affordable, ready, unblocked attack skill)` 26 times in seed 101 margin (13 legacy); fights end `error`/`timeout` with `mana=0..5`; 1 to 3.5 kills per minute. The caster archetype is `mana_burst` with `manaFloor` 0.35 (35 percent of the burst rate once the pool is empty); the observed floor is 0 until mana regenerates. Zone `Stony Field` is judged `margin_low` (0.9, low 0.6, need 1.5, `dps=11.5`) although the legacy arm farmed it 25 to 34 minutes per seed at 17.6 to 33.5 life/min without a death: the model is too cautious exactly where the data say the hero does fine, and too bold where the hero cannot kill.
- Balanced dial: the need line (1.5) is not the issue by itself; the two errors above are (per-kill time in a sparse, mana-starved zone, and the Stony Field margin). Power estimate: the OffenceDPS for the Sorceress (11.5 at clvl 12, `internal/power/skilldamage.json` for the attack the harness attaches) is plausible as a burst; what is missing is sustained, mana-limited and density-limited yield.
- Deficit arithmetic: 41 minutes at 34 xp/min instead of about 230 is about 8000 XP over three seeds against a per-seed shortfall of 3025 XP (about 9000 over three): the whole gap.

## 6. Proposed fixes
Done in this card: (a) abreport horizon (section 1); (b) one clock in the survival wiring (section 2).
Proposed, not done (each needs data or a decision):
1. **Realized yield in the Economy.** Replace the modelled kills per minute by a blend with the ledger's observed kills and XP per minute for the zone once it has `shrinkKills` kills (the ledger already holds `Kills`, `Seconds`, `XP` per row: `s.Kills/s.Seconds`); cap the prior of a zone with no data by the median realized yield of the zones tried (data: `z.Pay`, `danger` density). This is the fix for Burial Grounds (predicted 152, realized 34). Needs `Economy` and a unit test with two builds (caster and bruiser).
2. **Mana-limited kill speed.** `manaFloor` 0.35 to the observed ratio (0 to 0.1 with no potions and no regeneration) per archetype in `profiles.json` (archetype field, no class literal); recalibrate with the 26 watchdog events as a first sample.
3. **Dwell and fresh-game guard.** Do not take `fresh_game` into a zone whose `weakest` term is `offence_default` or `offence_weapon_class_default` while the merc is dead (hysteresis: the new zone's low margin must clear `enter` plus `switchMargin`); with seed 101 this keeps the hero in the cleared Dark Wood or moves to Black Marsh as legacy did.
4. **Attach the Paladin's attack to the harness hero** (BuildSpec for the A/B hero) so `src=skill`; the shadow then drops `kHeroUnreadMax` and the dps is the build's, not `defaultDpsPerClvl x clvl`. Harness change, not Go logic.
5. **Merc revive vs purse.** The model should approve a revive only when `Gain > Cost` and never let the legacy chore run on `!z.have`; log `merc: revive approved/left dead by the model` with the numbers (today only the suppress side logs).
6. **Slack for the gate.** The Paladin result (4 chickens vs 1) is small-number noise plus the defects above; do not tune the gate, rescreen.
