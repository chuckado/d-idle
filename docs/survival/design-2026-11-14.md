# Survival design: character power against zone danger (2026-11-14)

> status: design proposal, docs only (TASK-430, agent survivaldesign). Nothing here is implemented or decided. It answers [review-2026-11-14.md](review-2026-11-14.md); summary in [README.md](README.md). Every number tagged U is a starting value to be calibrated in shadow mode (slice S-B) before it decides anything.
>
> Tags: **V** verified (source named), **S** secondary, **I** inferred, **U** unverified.

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

Zone danger is a known quantity: the same monster sets, levels and pack sizes every game. Character power is what changes. So: describe each zone once, from the game tables, in physical units (how hard its packs hit, how long they take to kill, what they pay). Estimate the hero continuously in the same units (how much damage it can absorb, how fast it kills). Their ratio is one number, the **margin**. The bot farms the best-paying zone whose margin clears the player's **risk budget**, moves up when the margin grows (a level, a weapon, a full belt, a living merc) and steps down when it shrinks. Because the model will be wrong somewhere, a **ledger** of what each zone actually cost (potions, life dips, chickens, merc deaths) corrects it. Five concepts replace most of the 36 zone, exit, economy, merc and journey mechanisms of the review (the navigation skips, the failsafe ladder and the merc potion stay); the reflex layer (drink, chicken, crowd rule) stays exactly as it is.

```
   game tables (install)                hero snapshot (every plan step)
          |                                        |
   [ danger profile ]  per zone,          [ power estimate ]  life pool, sustain,
     generated, + overrides                 mitigation, kill speed, merc, escape
          |                                        |          ^
          +-------------->  [ margin ]  <----------+          |  archetype profile
                               |                              |  (how this kind of
        observed cost -->  [ ledger ]  corrects danger        |   character survives
        per zone             k_zone, k_hero                   |   and kills)
                               |
                         [ risk budget ]  one player dial
                               |
                 zone choice: best net XP per minute among zones with
                 margin >= budget; hysteresis; one "why" line per decision
```

## 2. Vocabulary (five concepts, proposed glossary terms)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Danger profile** | Per zone and difficulty: the reference encounters (typical pack, bad pack, elite pack, named bosses) with each monster's life, defence, damage per second, reach, speed and hazards, plus density and pay (XP and gold per kill). Generated from the install tables; never committed game data. |
| **Power estimate** | The hero now, in the same units: effective life pool, sustain per second, chance to be hit and blocked, resist factors, kill speed against a reference monster, merc contribution, escape tools; each input with a quality flag. |
| **Margin** | For one encounter: the life the hero can spend divided by the life the encounter takes while the hero kills it. 1.0 means "ends the fight with nothing left"; the zone margin is the margin of its bad pack. |
| **Risk budget** | The margin a zone must have before the hero farms it, and the observed cost it may run up (chickens, deaths, gold) before the hero leaves. One dial: Cautious, Balanced, Bold. |
| **Ledger** | Per zone, a decaying record of what it really cost and paid. It yields two correction factors: `k_zone` (this zone is harder or easier than the profile says) and `k_hero` (this hero is weaker or stronger than the estimate says, everywhere). |

Plus one supporting piece of data, the **archetype profile** (section 6): how a kind of character survives and kills, so one engine serves all classes without pretending they are the same.

## 3. Danger profile (the known quantity)

### 3.1 Content

One record per (area, difficulty), generated at run time from the install tables the bot already loads (`internal/data`: `Monster` carries life, defence, attack damage and to-hit per difficulty, resists, group sizes, velocity, melee and ranged flags; `internal/readiness/areatable.go` already walks levels and spawns). No new game data in the repo.

```json
{ "area": 6, "name": "Black Marsh", "diff": "normal",
  "mlvl": 6, "density": {"units": 215, "perScreen": 9.5},
  "monsters": [
    {"key": "goatman2", "share": 0.31, "life": 38, "def": 45, "toHit": 80,
     "dps": 6.1, "reach": "melee", "speed": 6, "group": [3,5], "hazards": []},
    {"key": "cr_archer2", "share": 0.18, "dps": 3.2, "reach": "ranged", "hazards": ["ranged"]},
    {"key": "fallenshaman2", "share": 0.04, "hazards": ["resurrect","fire"]}
  ],
  "encounters": {
    "typical": {"n": 6, "melee": 4, "ranged": 2},
    "bad":     {"n": 14, "melee": 9, "ranged": 5},
    "elite":   {"n": 7, "leader": "unique", "mods": ["extra_strong","extra_fast","cursed"], "rate": 0.031}
  },
  "bosses": [],
  "immune": [], "eliteImmune": [],
  "pay": {"xpPerKill": {"c8": 61, "c12": 37, "c16": 4}, "goldPerKill": 2.5},
  "source": {"tables": "V", "packs": "V run (12 seeds)", "dps": "I"} }
```

(The numbers in the example are placeholders to show the shape, U; the generator fills them.)

- `monsters`: from Levels, MonStats and MonLvl. `dps` per monster is (mean damage of its attacks) x (hit chance against a reference defence) / (attack period); the period model is the one of boss1-trace (animation frames plus the AI delay, I). The profile stores the raw terms so the margin can redo the hit chance against the real hero.
- `encounters`: pack sizes come from the population runs behind [../research/areamon-table.csv](../research/areamon-table.csv) (V run, 12 seeds: units, champions, uniques, minions per area) and the group columns of MonStats. `typical` is the median pack that reaches the hero together, `bad` the 90th percentile. The first version derives them from density and group size (I); slice S-B fits them to the `maxHostiles` and `remaining` fields the fight lines already log (V in the logs: Black Marsh up to 25, Cold Plains up to 6).
- `bosses`: superuniques and quest bosses with their mods and stats (boss1-trace has Act 1 Normal, V run): Rakanishu with 8 minions, the Smith with Extra Strong, Andariel with her poison rates.
- `hazards` are flags the archetype weighs (section 6): `ranged`, `fast`, `poison`, `resurrect`, `summon`, `curse`, `mana_burn`, `charge`, and element names.
- `pay`: XP per kill by clvl (the areamon table and the level-gap award, V) and gold per kill (xp-gold-trace, V run).

### 3.2 Generator and overrides

- Tool: `cmd/dangergen` (or a function next to `readiness.BuildAreaTable`, which it supersedes). Runs at bot start against the install, cached under the state directory with a hash of the table files, exactly as the extract cache does today.
- Hand-override layer: `internal/plandata/danger-overrides.json`, rows of `{area, diff, path, value, evidence, tag}`. Used where the tables cannot know: measured pack sizes, a d2d deviation (for example a monster pace difference), a superunique's real minion count. Every row cites its evidence file; a test fails a row whose evidence path does not exist.
- A committed **fixture** profile built from a tiny synthetic table set keeps tests offline (the pattern of `readiness_test.go`). Real-install anchors run where an install exists (`TestRealAnchors` pattern).
- In sync by construction: nothing in the profile is typed by hand except overrides.

## 4. Power estimate (the thing that changes)

Recomputed on every plan step and on every change of level, equipment, belt, skill points or merc state. All terms are in life points and seconds.

| Term | Formula (first version) | Inputs today | Quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Life pool `L` | `maxLife x (1 - chickenPct)` : the life the hero may spend before the reflex layer pulls it out | `d2.Player.MaxLife` (V), knob | measured |
| Potion pool `P` | healing potions in belt and pack that can reach the belt x heal amount of their tier x class factor | belt read (V), potion tables ([../potions.md](../potions.md), decomp10) | measured |
| Sustain `s` | life regeneration + a healing aura or skill (from the build's skills) + potion flow cap (one potion effect at a time: heal amount / effect seconds, the R4 rule) | skills, potion effect model | derived |
| Chance to be hit `h(m)` | `toHit / (toHit + defence) x 2 mlvl / (mlvl + clvl)`, clamped 5 to 95 percent (S sk-melee-trace, used by boss1-trace) | defence: **not read today** (the `minute` line prints `armor: ?`) | default until read |
| Block `b` | block chance when a shield is worn (playerdef-trace); hitrec-trace: block and flinch length per class | equipment, class tables | derived |
| Damage factors `r(e)` | physical: reductions; elements: (100 - resist) / 100 with the difficulty penalty | `d2.Player.Resist` (V read; penalty basis U, TASK-216) | measured, basis U |
| Kill speed `q(m)` | damage per second of the build's active attack against monster `m` (its defence, resists, life): the model of [../research/pal-skill-matrix.md](../research/pal-skill-matrix.md) section 4 generalised by archetype (section 6); for mana-gated attacks `min(burst dps, mana income / mana per damage)` | weapon, stats, skill levels (V reads), skill tables | derived; measured once fights exist |
| Merc `q_m`, `a_m` | merc dps from its level and weapon (mercstats-trace), and the share of melee attackers it holds while alive; both 0 when dead | merc state (V since mercdump) | derived |
| Escape `e` | has town portal; cast interruptible; teleport; the chicken always works | belt, skills | measured |

**Honest uncertainty.** Each term carries `quality`: `measured` (read from the game this step), `derived` (computed from tables and a read), `default` (not readable yet). The estimate is a pair (best, low); `low` applies a discount per non-measured term (U: derived 0.85, default 0.6). Zone entry uses `low`, staying uses `best` (section 5.3). This makes "we do not read defence" visible as a cost instead of a silent assumption, and it shrinks by itself when the read lands.

**Self-measurement.** The bot already logs seconds and kills per fight. After about 20 kills against a monster type the measured kill time replaces the modelled `q(m)` for that type (and feeds `k_hero`, section 5.4). So the offence model only has to be roughly right at a new zone's door.

## 5. Margin, risk budget, feedback

### 5.1 Margin of one encounter

The hero kills an encounter of `n` monsters one after another (or several at once, by the archetype's area factor `A`). Monster `i` takes `t_i = life_i / (q(m_i) + q_m)` seconds. While `j` monsters are alive, the damage per second arriving is the sum over the attackers that can reach the hero:

```
D_in(j) = sum over reachable attackers of  dps_m x h(m) x (1 - b) x r(e_m) x w(hazards_m)
reachable melee = min(j_melee x (1 - a_m), contactCap)     ranged: all alive, in range
taken  = sum over the kill order of  D_in(j) x t_j / A
budget = L + P_usable + s x (total kill time)
margin = budget / taken
```

`contactCap` is how many melee monsters can stand around the hero (8 on open ground, fewer in corridors, per archetype exposure). `w` is the archetype's hazard weight (1 unless a hazard matters more or less for this kind of character). `P_usable` is the potions the effect rule lets the hero drink during the fight.

For identical monsters this reduces to `taken = d x t x n(n+1)/2`: **danger grows with the square of the pack size and linearly with the kill time**. That is the term every clvl rule misses. V logs (review section 4.1): Cold Plains, about 5 attackers at 1.5 s per kill, 22 d-seconds; Black Marsh, about 10 in reach at 9 s per kill, 495 d-seconds, at a "level gap" of minus 6.

The zone margin is reported as three numbers: `typical`, `bad` (the one that gates), `elite` (with the pack danger rules of today deciding per pack whether to engage; the elite margin tells the tactical layer when to avoid).

Derived from the same sum, per zone: **potions per kill** (the part of `taken` above what sustain covers, divided by the heal amount), **net gold per minute** (gold per kill x kills per minute, minus potions x price, minus expected merc revives x quote, minus repair), and **XP per minute** (kills per minute from kill time and density, the levelsim supply model, x XP per kill).

### 5.2 Risk budget (one dial)

| Dial | Enter when bad-pack margin (low estimate) is at least | Stay while margin (best estimate, ledger-corrected) is at least | Observed cost that forces a step down |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cautious | 2.0 | 1.6 | any chicken or merc death in the zone |
| Balanced (default) | 1.5 | 1.2 | 2 chickens or merc deaths per 30 min in the zone, or any death |
| Bold | 1.2 | 1.0 | a death |

All six numbers are U; S-B calibrates them so that Balanced reproduces the zones the logged runs survived without potions (Cold Plains at clvl 4 to 5, h4: 4 potions per 100 kills) and refuses the ones they did not (Black Marsh at clvl 12 without a merc, swap7). The dial replaces the stance row's safety half, `bot.levelMargin`, `bot.leaveSlack`, the readiness strictness proposal and the levelsim modes `plan`, `strict`, `bold`. Quest bosses use the same dial with the journey's wording (section 7).

### 5.3 Zone choice, ratchet and hysteresis

Every plan step (and at once after a level-up, an equipment change, a merc change or a ledger event):

1. Candidates: areas the hero can reach (waypoints, quest state), not navigation-faulted. Navigation faults keep their own short skip (X8 of the review); they are no longer mixed with danger.
2. For each: margin, XP per minute, net gold per minute, travel minutes.
3. Eligible: bad-pack margin >= the dial's enter line (the current zone: the stay line).
4. Score: `value = xpPerMin + lambda x netGoldPerMin`, amortised over the expected stay: `value x stay / (stay + travel)`. `lambda` is 0 while the purse covers the kit and the next purchase; it rises as the purse falls short (kit, revive quote, repair), so a broke hero prefers gold without a separate mode.
5. Pick the best. Switch only if the best beats the current zone by a switching margin (U 15 percent) or the current zone fell under its stay line or is depleted (the levelsim supply term: remaining share under the plan's floor).

Properties:

- **Ratchets up by itself.** A level adds life and damage, so every zone's margin rises and the next zone crosses the enter line. No entry level is written anywhere. A better weapon, a filled belt or a revived merc does the same within the same level; a dead merc or an empty belt lowers it.
- **No flapping.** The enter line is above the stay line; entry uses the low estimate and staying uses the best; a zone left for cost carries its `k_zone` (section 5.4) and cannot be re-entered until the hero's power or the ledger changed.
- **No parking.** There is no ban. A zone is ineligible only with numbers ("bad pack 14, margin 0.6, need 1.5"), and the first field of the act always has the highest margin, so the candidate set cannot be empty for a hero that can fight at all. If every margin is under the Bold line, the hero is in a real hole (naked, no potions): the result is the first field plus a "needs you" line, which is today's gear gate HIGH, now with a reason.
- **Travel and town time is a term, not the driver.** It only amortises; a 2-minute walk matters for a 5-minute stay and not for a 30-minute one. Depletion and restarts stay with the levelsim supply model.
- **Thin belt, town trips.** Potions are in the budget, so a drained belt lowers the current zone's margin under the stay line; the choice becomes "town, then the best zone", or a lower zone when the purse cannot fill the belt. The trip triggers E1 to E3 of the review reduce to that one test plus the pack-full and repair reasons.

### 5.4 Ledger: observed risk as feedback

Per (zone, difficulty), exponentially decaying over field time in that zone (half-life U 20 min) and persisted in the state directory:

`fightSeconds, kills, damageTaken, healingPotions, minLifePct histogram, fightsUnder50, chickens, portals, deaths, mercDeaths, maxHostiles, xp, goldIn, goldOut`.

All of it is already in the log lines `combat fight`, `combat potion`, `safety decision`, `merc: died`, `area summary`, `minute` (V, the review's section 4 was computed from them).

Two corrections:

- `k_zone = observed taken per kill / predicted taken per kill`, shrunk toward 1 with few kills (weight = kills / (kills + 30), U). It multiplies the zone's predicted `taken`. Hard events move it at once: a chicken or a merc death multiplies `k_zone` by 1.5, a death by 2.5 (U), so the stay line is crossed without waiting for an average.
- `k_hero = observed kill time / predicted kill time`, pooled over all zones: the offence model's error for this character. It moves every zone's margin, so a hero whose weapon is worse than the model thinks is held back everywhere, not zone by zone.

Decay is the forgiveness: `k_zone` relaxes toward 1 with time away **and** is recomputed when the power estimate changes by more than 10 percent, because "the zone was too hard for the hero I was" says little about the hero after two levels. That is the "do not come back until something changed" rule the review found missing.

A zone that pays more than it costs and whose observed margin runs well above the stay line pulls nothing down; the hero advances when the next zone's own margin passes the enter line, which the pooled `k_hero` (a hero killing faster than modelled) brings forward. So the controller both backs off and advances.

Observed pack at the door: when the hero leaves a zone with hostiles around (today's stale pack note), that pack is a known encounter at a known spot. Its margin is computed with the same function; re-entry by the portal that stands in it is refused while its margin is under the enter line (walk in from the waypoint or edge instead, or start a fresh game). This replaces the four stale-pack knobs.

### 5.5 Mercenary, honestly

- Alive: `q_m` adds to kill speed and `a_m` takes attackers off the hero. Dead: both 0, so margins fall and the hero steps down by the normal rule. This replaces `no_merc_add`, the with-merc and without-merc floor pairs and `MercAliveGate`.
- Revive is a purchase with a return: `gain = (best eligible value with merc - best eligible value without) x expected minutes until he dies again`; expected minutes come from the ledger's merc deaths in the zone the hero would farm (no data: the class table's prior). Revive when `gain x lambda-adjusted worth > quote` and the purse rule of the kit allows it.
- Dying repeatedly is a signal about the zone, not about the revive: two merc deaths in one zone within 30 min raise that zone's `k_zone` (above) and set "no revive for this zone": the hero farms a zone where the margin holds without him, or one where he survives. swap7 would have paid 360 gold once, not twice plus a refused third.
- Merc potions and gear stay as they are (M3, docs/merc-gear.md).

### 5.6 Economy

Net gold per minute is part of the score (5.3 step 4). A zone that burns more in potions and revives than it drops is negative and loses to a cheaper zone as soon as `lambda` is above 0. Recovery mode, poverty mode, the kit gate and the gold farm become that one term: the hero that cannot afford its kit has thin potions (lower margin, so only easier zones are eligible) and a high `lambda` (so among them the gold-rich one wins). The purchase order in town (potions before portal before revive) and the stash rule are unchanged.

## 6. Archetypes: one engine, a few kinds of character

Not one formula that pretends all classes are the same, and not seven hand-built models: the engine (sections 3 to 5) is shared, and an **archetype profile** tells it how this kind of character survives and kills. A class and build map to an archetype in data; a thin override layer exists for the cases evidence demands.

### 6.1 Two axes, five named archetypes

What differs between classes, for this model, is only two things: how the hero is *exposed* (who can hit it) and how it *deals damage* (what limits it). Each archetype is a preset over those two axes.

| Archetype | Exposure | Offence | Classes and builds today |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bruiser` | contact: stands in the pack; defence, block and life pool decide | sustained melee, no resource gate; may hit several (Zeal, Frenzy) | Paladin Zeal and Sacrifice (the default test class), Barbarian, melee Druid and Assassin forms |
| `ranged_physical` | range: melee attackers reach it only after closing; kites | sustained missile, ammunition and attack rating | Amazon bow and javelin |
| `caster` | range, low life; may have a shield skill or teleport | burst, mana-gated; area skills; subject to immunities | Sorceress (the second test class), fire and wind Druid, Bone Necromancer |
| `summoner` | screened: pets hold the attackers; the hero's own pool is small | pet damage, ramps with pet count, slow start; curses | summon Necromancer, summon Druid |
| `hybrid_tank_caster` | contact (shield, block) | mana-gated area damage | Hammerdin, trap Assassin (contact optional) |

`hybrid` is not a fifth model: it is a preset that takes its exposure from one row and its offence from another. A new mix is a new preset in data, not code.

### 6.2 The fields an archetype sets (exact list)

```
{ "id": "caster",
  "exposure": {
    "mode": "range",            // contact | range | screened
    "contactCap": 3,            // melee attackers that reach the hero at once while it plays its range
    "closeSeconds": 2.5,        // time a melee monster needs to close; ranged dps applies from second 0
    "screenShare": 0.0,         // share of attackers held by pets or the merc beyond the merc's own a_m
    "defenceCounts": false,     // whether armour defence and block enter h(m) (contact) or are ignored
    "secondPool": "none"        // none | mana (an energy-shield style pool counts toward L)
  },
  "offence": {
    "mode": "mana_burst",       // sustained | mana_burst | pet
    "areaFactor": "skill",      // 1 | skill (targets hit per action from the skill row) | pets
    "resource": "mana",         // none | mana | ammo
    "rampSeconds": 0,           // time to reach full damage (pets)
    "immunityBlocks": true      // an immune monster's kill time is the fallback attack's, or infinite
  },
  "hazardWeights": {"ranged": 1.3, "fast": 1.6, "mana_burn": 2.0, "poison": 1.0,
                    "resurrect": 1.0, "charge": 1.5, "curse": 1.0},
  "escape": {"portalInterruptible": true, "mobilitySkill": "from_build"},
  "uncertainty": {"derived": 0.85, "default": 0.6} }
```

Eleven parameters in three groups plus the hazard weights. What each means for the two test classes:

- **Paladin (`bruiser`).** Exposure `contact`, `contactCap` 8, `defenceCounts` true: survivability is life pool x chance to be hit x block. Offence `sustained`, `areaFactor` from the skill (Zeal retargets inside the cast, pal-skill-matrix), no resource gate. Hazard weights near 1 for melee packs; `ranged` 1.2 (must walk to archers, V: "Shaman out of melee reach" 23 fallbacks in swap7). The dangers that matter: big melee packs and slow kills (the Black Marsh case).
- **Sorceress (`caster`).** Exposure `range`, `contactCap` 3, `closeSeconds` from monster speed: survivability is "kill or slow them before they arrive", then a small life pool; `fast` and `charge` weigh 1.5 to 1.6 because they erase the range. Offence `mana_burst`: kill speed is the lesser of the skill's damage rate and what the mana pool and potions sustain (`resource.castsPerMonster`, `restStartManaPct` become outputs of this, not knobs); `immunityBlocks` true. The dangers that matter: being reached, running dry, immunes, mana burn.

### 6.3 Derived, not typed

Most archetype inputs come from data the bot already has, so a new class or build slots in without code:

| Parameter | Derived from |
|---|---|
| life per level and per vitality, mana, stamina | CharStats (V read in boss1-trace: Paladin LifePerLevel 8 and LifePerVitality 12 in fourths) and the build table's stat rows |
| which attack, its damage type, mana cost, targets per action, range | the build's `main_attack` or the profile's `bands[].attack` list and Skills.txt rows |
| interruptible or not (flinch matters or not) | Skills.txt `interrupt` column (hitrec-trace: Zeal, Smite, Charge, Jab, Whirlwind are not interruptible, V run) |
| block and flinch length | class tables (hitrec-trace: Paladin 10 ticks, Sorceress 16) |
| contact or range | the combat profile's `melee` block (present for Paladin and Barbarian, V `internal/combat/classdata/profiles.json`), else the main attack's range |
| pets | summon skills in the build rows |
| escape | Teleport or a movement skill in the build |

**Default for a new class or build (nearest archetype).** The mapping rule, as data-driven code with no class names: main attack is a melee-range skill and the profile has a `melee` block -> `bruiser`; main attack is a weapon missile -> `ranged_physical`; main attack costs mana and is not weapon-based -> `caster`; more than a third of the build's skill points go to summon skills -> `summoner`; contact plus mana-gated area attack -> `hybrid_tank_caster`. A build file may name its archetype explicitly (`"archetype": "bruiser"`), which wins. An unknown class or a build the rule cannot place is an error that names it (AGENTS.md: never a silent fallback to the Sorceress).

Tests that prove it:

- `TestEveryBuildResolvesArchetype`: every `internal/plandata/build-*.json` resolves to an archetype, and the result equals a golden table (so a change is a visible diff).
- `TestSyntheticClassGetsNearestArchetype`: four made-up builds (a melee skill, a bow skill, a mana spell, a summon set) on a fake class resolve to the four base archetypes with no code change.
- `TestMarginMonotone`, per archetype: more life, more damage, a living merc, a fuller belt never lower a margin; a bigger pack, a faster or harder-hitting monster never raise it.
- `TestTwoBuilds`: every margin and zone-choice test runs for the Paladin and the Sorceress (the Build flexibility rule).

### 6.5 The override layer and its policy

File: `internal/plandata/power-overrides.json`, rows of `{scope: class | build, id, path, value, evidence, tag, review_after}`.

An override is allowed only when all of these hold:

1. The fact is real and the tables cannot yield it: a V trace, or logged fights (at least 200 kills over at least 3 zones) in which the archetype's prediction is off by more than 30 percent **and** `k_hero` did not absorb it (it stayed outside 0.7 to 1.4).
2. It is a data row with an `evidence` path that exists (test) and a `review_after` date; an expired row fails the check until re-confirmed.
3. It is not something the ledger learns by itself within an hour of play.
4. At most five rows per class (a sixth means the archetype is wrong: fix or split the archetype).

First rows the evidence already supports:

| Scope | Override | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| build `paladin_normal30_v2` | Sacrifice costs 8 percent of the damage dealt in own life: add to `taken` while it is the active attack | hitrec-trace Q4 (V read, V run on the arithmetic); the profile's `lifePrice` block |

## 7. Journey and quests: goals with a margin, not a level

- Quests stay goals in `data/campaign/normal.json`, in the journey's order. "Warn, never wall" (ADR 0011) stays.
- A chapter's band is computed from the margin of its boss encounter (and of the zone on the way): **too early** under the Bold line, **ready** between Bold and the dial's enter line, **comfortable** above it. The journey panel shows the number and the weakest term: "Andariel: margin 0.9 (needs 1.5). Poison takes 45 percent of your pool; 3 antidotes add 0.3; the Rogue adds 0.4."
- The chapter's `levels` rows, the `bossGates` level column, the route guard floors and the class rows of `boss_levels.go` remain as a **legacy floor** during S-B to S-D (shown beside the margin), and are removed per boss in S-E when the margin has agreed with the logged outcomes.
- The kit part of the boss gates (potions, portal, antidotes) is not a separate gate: those items are in the budget of the boss margin, so "buy 3 antidotes" appears as the cheapest way to raise it.
- Unattended holds (the throwaway fight rule) and Pact consent are policy, not survival, and are untouched.

## 8. Explainable and testable

### 8.1 One "why" per decision

Log line on every zone decision (and on every refusal that changes the plan):

```

  not Black Marsh: margin 0.6 (bad pack 14, 9.0 s/kill, k_zone 2.4 after merc death x2)   not Dark Wood: margin 1.3 < 1.5
```

API (extends `/api/glance` of the UI redesign, [../ui/redesign-2026-11-14/G-build-plan.md](../ui/redesign-2026-11-14/G-build-plan.md) slice S2; `why` uses the `{code, text}` shape of C-orders.md):

"zone": {"id": 4, "name": "Stony Field", "margin": 1.9, "marginLow": 1.6, "need": 1.5,
         "xpPerMin": 311, "netGoldPerMin": 4, "dial": "balanced",
         "why": {"code": "best_pay_eligible", "text": "best pay among 4 safe zones"}},

  {"id": 6, "name": "Black Marsh", "margin": 0.6, "xpPerMin": 455,

The Status sentence reads `zone.why.text`; "Why here" is `zone`; "Why not X" is `alternatives[]`; the risk meter's rows become `power` and the margin terms. Codes are a closed list in one file so the UI and the hero voice can phrase them.

### 8.2 Tests and simulation

- **Unit**: the margin is a pure function `Margin(power, encounter, archetype) -> (margin, terms)`. Tests use fake power and fake encounters; the monotonicity and two-build tests of 6.3; golden files for the "why" text.
- **Replay** (`levelsim -shadow LOG`): feeds a recorded log's `minute` rows (clvl, life, belt, gold, merc) and fight lines through the estimator and prints, per minute, the zone the new system would pick and its margin for the zone the hero was actually in. This is the validation tool of S-B and S-C.
- **Simulation**: `tools/levelsim` today has "survival: not simulated; danger enters only as entry floors" (levelsim.md section 2). Extension: replace `Env.Floor` and the three modes by the margin with the dial; add potions, gold and the merc to the state; add a cost per kill from `taken`. Then the optimiser answers the real question (XP per hour subject to a risk budget), and the 53.7k against 91.7k against 102k comparison becomes a property of the dial instead of three unverified floor tables.
- **Live**: a drill per archetype on a hidden instance (checklist in the slices), zero setup writes for anything that is evidence about behaviour (provenance rule).

| Inputs not readable (defence, damage, block) | quality flags; the estimate is discounted and says so in "weakest"; the read is a prerequisite card of S-B |
