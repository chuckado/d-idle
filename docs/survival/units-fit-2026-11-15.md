# Units per clear: the danger generator against game.exe's room population (Acts 1 and 2, Normal)

> status: current (2026-11-15, card decomp28). Tags: V run (game.exe's room population and room list under the emulator, 12 seeds, docs/emu-image.md), D (Levels.txt / LvlMaze.txt), I (the fit). Trace and reasoning: [docs/research/decomp28-trace.md](../research/decomp28-trace.md) section 7. Data: `docs/research/data28/areas-input.csv`, per-seed files `data28/perseed-act1.csv`, `perseed-act2.csv`; the data file for the generator `internal/danger/areaunits.json` (unused until a wiring card). Reproduce: `python3 tools/emu/dc28_fit.py report` (stdlib, no emulator).

## Result

- The shipped `units = MonDen x groupMean x k(DrlgType)` has a 94 percent median error over the 67 usable areas (the 41 percent of `TestAreamonDiff` is Act 1 alone; Act 2 alone is 147 percent). Worst single areas: Inner Cloister +719 percent, Tower Cellar 4 +708 percent, Claw Viper 2 +702 percent.
- `units = tiles x exp(a [+ b ln MonDen])`, tiles = the rooms game.exe's generator builds times 64 (every room is 8 x 8): median 16 percent in sample, 17 percent leaving each area out, 17 percent leaving each family out (Tal Rasha's seven tombs, the Catacombs, ... together). By type: mazes 12 / 13 / 14, outdoors 18 / 19 / 19, presets 50 / 54 / 54 (no model: the monsters of a preset map come from the map).
- Fit on Act 1 only, predicting Act 2: median 48 percent (mazes 55): the MonDen exponent is extrapolated from 600 to 1,056 up to 2,200. The 17 percent needs Act 2 in the training set.
- Parameters (all 67 areas): mazes `ln(units/tile) = -5.8175 + 0.4111 ln MonDen`; presets `-3.1964`; outdoors `-3.6156`.
- Missing terms in the shipped formula: the real area (a per-area tile count), a per-type rate with MonDen only in mazes; the mean pack size is not a driver (exponent 0.03 to 0.1). Unique packs are inside the units (about 1 to 3 uniques and 1 to 10 minions per clear in most areas).
- `Zone.Density.PerScreen` divides by the Levels.txt size (`generate.go:186`): 200 x 200 for mazes, so mazes show 0.4 to 3 units per screen where game.exe's area gives 10 to 22.

## Hold-out and overfitting

Four checks, none uses its own area when it predicts it: leave-one-area-out (67 refits), leave-one-family-out (area names with the level number stripped), Act 1 fit against Act 2, and the fact that both rate models have at most two parameters per type (three types, four parameters). The den exponent is the only term that is not a constant, and it comes from 44 mazes at MonDen 600, 680, 990, 1,024, 1,056 and 2,200.
