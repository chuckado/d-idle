# Replay of the sandbox hero's log through the survival shadow and controller (2026-11-14)

> status: current (card survreplay, TASK-458). First run of the S-B margin and the S-C ledger/controller on a REAL log. Calibration report only: NOTHING in `params.json`, `profiles.json` or any shipped default was changed. Tags: V verified (how), S secondary, I inferred, U unverified.

## Input

- The retained log of the sandbox container (about 7.8 hours, 2026-11-14 15:03 to 22:52 UTC, hero Paladin on the `bruiser` archetype, clvl 11 to 14, Normal Act 1, one death, 11 chickens, 12 merc deaths). Pulled read-only with one `docker logs` pipe, compressed on the sandbox host, scratch kept off the repository (V: nothing was written there; the host listing after the pull showed no file and no process of ours).
- The archived hero runs (`play-archive-*`) hold only state files (`state.tgz`: character save, ladder state, d2d output), no bot log, so they were listed and NOT copied.
- Fixture: `internal/survival/testdata/replay-sandbox-clvl11to14.txt` (about 300 KB, 3090 lines, text `key=value` format that `levelsim -shadow` reads; named `.txt` because the repository ignores `*.log`). Kept: `zone entry`, `area summary`, `planner` (area only), `area skipped`, `minute` (selected fields), `safety decision`, `character died` (area, killer, life), `merc: died`, `merc: seen` (alive, level), `combat potion`, and one `combat fight` line per fight (area, kills, damage, potions, seconds). Dropped: instance names, positions, item and inventory text, navigation traces, free text. `go run ./cmd/leakcheck -mode fail` is clean (see the card).
- Full outputs of the three dials (rows included): `internal/survival/testdata/replay-sandbox-clvl11to14.out.txt`.

## Commands (V: run on the builder, one batched job, exit 0)

`go run ./tools/levelsim/cmd/levelsim -controller internal/survival/testdata/replay-sandbox-clvl11to14.txt -install /gamedata -arch bruiser -dial balanced` (and `cautious`, `bold`). `-controller` is new (`tools/levelsim/controller.go`): it prints the S-B stay table and pass rule first, then the controller replay. `LEVELSIM_ROWS=1` adds the uncollapsed decision rows.

How the controller replay works (the same hooks as `cmd/d2bot/survivalwire.go`): every fight, chicken, death, merc death and area summary is observed into a `survival.Ledger` at the log's own timestamps; `Choose` runs at every `minute` line and at every `planner` pick that was not followed by `area skipped`; candidates are the 11 Act 1 areas the planner named in the log, judged by the same margin and the same Economy; reachability is NOT modelled (the planner marked most of its picks "unreachable" at times). The hero is rebuilt from the log (clvl, max life, healing potions, merc alive and level); defence, block, resist and weapon stay default (the log has none, same limit as S-B).

## Result in one paragraph

The S-B pass rule FAILS on this log (balanced: bad stays under the enter line 6 of 14, quiet stays above it 8 of 8). The controller passes the S-C rule for the death sequence: after the second merc death in Black Marsh it leaves and does not name Black Marsh again for 2 h 31 min at the same power (the real planner re-entered it 4 times in the next 35 minutes), it never produces an empty candidate set (0 holes in 445 minutes, all three dials), and it refuses the second revive of the sequence. But it is far more timid than what survived (k_hero 6.2 is the replay missing offence, see below), picks Cave Level 1 in 163 of 445 minutes, and agrees with the planner's picks in 2 of 45.

## What disagrees with what happened (and the constants that look wrong)

1. `k_hero` ends at 6.2 and `k_zone` at 4.3 (Blood Moor) and 7.3 (Cold Plains): replay artefact plus a real hazard (I). The log has no weapon or defence, so the model's kill time is the default offence and the ledger correction absorbs all of it (observed 1.7 to 2.7 seconds per kill, model well under one); at the same time the observed damage per kill is 0.3 to 3 life, so on trivial zones the ratio of two tiny numbers hits the clamp. Effect: every margin is divided by 6, most zones read `risky` (249 of 445 minutes), the shadow retreats to Cave Level 1 and the first fields. Proposals (not applied): (a) a floor on predicted damage per kill in `k_zone` (about 1 life per kill, I) so near-zero predictions cannot inflate it; (b) `kMax` 8 to 3 (evidence: the hero lived 7.8 h with one death while k sat at 6); (c) the real fix is the one S-B already asked for: add `dps`, `def`, `block` to the `zone entry` line so `k_hero` starts near 1.
2. Merc term: the S-B replay builds the merc with level 0 (the entry line has none). The controller replay reads `merc: seen level=`; both still call Tamoe Highland `risky`, not `too hard`, although the merc died in 5 of 5 visits within two minutes. The ledger does learn it (hard events), but the per-visit gap (30 to 60 minutes) is longer than the window (`windowMinutes` 30), so `ForcedOut` never fires for balanced (`either` 2 within the window). Proposal: count merc deaths over `hardHalfLifeMinutes` (120) instead of 30 for the closing rule, or lower `either` to 1 for a zone that killed him in under 3 minutes (I).
3. `mercPriorMinutes` 40: measured median merc life 25 minutes (11 intervals, one hero; 4 of 11 under 21): set to 25 (S).
4. Revive quote: the constant 400 against the real `shop`/`npc` lines: not readable in this log (U). The revive verdict is stingier than the player (revived 12 of 12): the value is `valuePerGold` 0.5 (U); no evidence to move it from one hero, but it is the knob to look at in the A/B (S-D).
5. Pay check (observed against the model, area summaries of 2+ minutes): the kill rates are close (observed 9 to 19 kills per minute against 12 to 15 predicted); XP per minute: Dark Wood 411 against 273, Black Marsh 381 against 560, Cave Level 1 162 against 11, Cave Level 2 523 against 11, Stony Field 256 against 242, Tamoe 709 against 841. The two Cave rows are the area summaries of stays that included quest travel and corpse trips counted against the level-gap model (I), but Cave 1 is also the shadow's favourite pick on a prediction of 11 XP per minute, which only makes sense because the margin leaves it as one of the few eligible zones. Watch before S-D.
6. Planner fall-through: the planner's own "unreachable" skips (burst of 10 areas at 19:20, 19:57, 20:22, 20:46, 21:23, 21:57) show the planner falling through its whole list to Blood Moor at clvl 14 for 10 to 22 minutes each time. Out of scope here (navigation), but the shadow was right to disagree there (Blood Moor pays 3 XP per minute at clvl 14, 8 of the 8 `good` stays are Blood Moor or Cold Plains).

## Limits of this replay

- One hero, one build (Paladin, `bruiser`), Normal Act 1, 7.8 hours. The "good" class is only trivial fields.
- No defence, block, resist, weapon: the S-B limit; `k_hero` is therefore not a clean calibration (item 1).
- Candidates are the planner's list; no waypoint or quest reachability, no depleted marks, no gold purse beyond the log's `gold` field.
- The `safety decision` lines carry the zone but the deaths in town trips are attributed to the zone shown in the decision (Black Marsh), as the bot hook would.
- The merc-death hook has no zone; the replay attributes it to the last entered area (the town while in town: the last farm zone).
