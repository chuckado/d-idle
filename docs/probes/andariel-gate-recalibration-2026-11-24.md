## 3. Why the hero needs k = 3 (the weapon), a separate row
The ledger's k_hero is the ratio of observed kill time to the model's. The model's offence for the live hero is read from the character (stats 21/22 and skills); the worn weapon is the starting one (class 25 in the worn log, a Short Sword: damage 2 to 7, average 4.5; a rare Scepter, damage about 10 to 18 before affixes, sits unidentified in the vault, "equip: rare Scepter not worn: unidentified (back in the vault)"). The fixture hero uses an average of 3+L to 8+2L (35 at clvl 20), about 3.5 times the Short Sword's 9 to 10 with strength: the same factor as the clamp (S, arithmetic, not measured on the bot). So the 3x is the weapon (and the skill used: a Paladin with Zeal and a Short Sword does about 10 per hit): a real weapon from the vault or the plan's pickup rules takes k toward 1.5 to 2, and with the plan's body armour (defence 30 to 140, a factor 2 at most in the same model) the board word passes in the human range. The floor is not the thing to move first. Row for the plan: "equip the vault weapon and body armour (Wear set fixed 2026-11-24 13:32Z), watch k_hero and the minute line `hitsTaken`, then apply the floors above".
