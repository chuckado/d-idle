# postfix-soak2 analysis: root causes of F1 to F6, the walking share and a ranked fix-card list (card soak2study)

> status: current. Offline study of the finished Gate 1 soak 2 ([postfix-soak2.md](postfix-soak2.md), origin/main 0b0bdb2, one seed, 2 h wall = 21.25 virtual h), read from the full bot log of the worker host (11.9 MB, 1,275 `minute` lines) and compared with the full log of soak 1 ([postfix-soak1.md](postfix-soak1.md)). No game, no slot, no product code touched. Tags: VERIFIED = counted from the logs or reproduced by an offline test, INFERRED = read from code or derived from VERIFIED numbers, UNKNOWN = the evidence does not decide. Times are VIRTUAL seconds unless marked wall (a virtual second is the lockstep game clock; killtime and minute lines are virtual, log timestamps are wall, about 10.6 virtual per wall second).

## Summary (the five things that matter)
1. **Walking is search and travel, not approach walking (VERIFIED).** The `killtime` "walk" bucket is "player in walk/run mode or moved", and it includes town walking, area travel and exploration. 30% of all kill time (22.7k of 76.6k s, 77 kills) sits in gaps of 160 s or more between two kills, and 62% in gaps over 40 s. Per-kill approach walking (gaps under 5 s) is 6.7% of the time. The 70% is therefore where the bot is not fighting: town trips (33% of kill time contains one), exploration of areas without monsters, area hopping and stuck rungs.
2. **The XP/h gap is mostly levels and poverty (VERIFIED numbers, INFERRED weights).** 62% of the minutes are at clvl 11 to 13 where the run earns 56 XP/min (soak 1: 67) against 147 XP/min below clvl 11. "Recovery: farming gold" is 34% of the minutes in soak 2 (soak 1: 23%); gold is the binding constraint: 35.2k gold spent in the run, 52% of it on 184 town portal scrolls, 39% on 231 Minor Mana Potions, 8% on healing.
3. **F4 is a navigator bug, but it explains almost none of the time (VERIFIED, offline repro).** `nav: no path: empty path` is a planner result for "goal is blocked, nearest walkable cell is where I stand", which is a success mis-reported as a failure, and `Fighter.valid` accepts exactly these points. 90% of the 1,191 failures come from the out-of-mana back-off. Direct cost about 71 s (0.1% of kill time); indirect cost: each failed back-off burns one of the 8 back-off steps.
4. **F1 was not an empty belt at entry (VERIFIED, corrects the soak report).** The belt held 4 healing and 3 mana potions when the bot entered Burial Grounds at clvl 4 (maximum life about 40) with 155 gold left. Four healing potions were drunk in about 16 virtual s against a crowd of five undead; then the town portal cast at 34% life was cut by melee hits and the scroll was spent. The empty belt is the effect. The belt-window FAIL (27 lines) is the poverty after that death (gold 0, 25 virtual min to 16 gold), not the start of the run.
5. **F6 is already answered (VERIFIED, stale in the soak report).** `walking=` is "moving", not "walking instead of running": docs/probes/drills/README.md (live, in both modes). The soak report's UNVERIFIED tag can be dropped.

## 1. F4: combat `no path: empty path` (1,185 of 1,191 move failures; soak 1: 1,362)
**Code path (VERIFIED, read).** `nav.walk` returns `ErrNoPath: empty path` when `planResult.wps` is empty (internal/nav/walk.go, after the plan). `planHard` returns empty `wps` with no error when the A* path has one cell, because `resample` returns nil for fewer than two points (internal/nav/smooth.go). One cell happens when the goal is known and unwalkable, is snapped to the nearest walkable cell (`res.snapped`), and that cell is the start cell. The "snapped goal already reached" shortcut at the top of the walk loop runs BEFORE the first plan (it needs `w.have`), so the first iteration reaches the empty-wps error.
**Offline repro (VERIFIED, run on the worker host, test not committed).** A one-cell-wide corridor, player in it, goal 12, 8, 5 cells into the wall: `plan` gives `wps=0 err=nil snapped=true cells=1` for all four goals; `Navigator.GoTo` returns `nav: no path: empty path`; `Navigator.PathTo` returns `nil` error. `Fighter.valid` (internal/combat/fight.go) only checks `PathTo` error, so `awayPoint` (internal/combat/position.go) picks the blocked point as the best retreat direction and the move then fails.
**Callers (VERIFIED, from the log).** The log line before the failure is `combat out of mana: backing off` in 1,074 of 1,191 cases (90%), `combat dodging` 55, flee/crowd/kite the rest; the next line is another back-off in 880 cases or `back-off cap, melee` in 143. Failures never come in runs (1,185 single, 3 pairs): the next tick picks another point or melee takes over. Back-offs in the run: 3,872 (soak 1: 6,040), 28% of them fail.
**Where (VERIFIED, area of the nearest minute line).** Stony Field (area 4) 554, Cave Level 1 (9) 368, Crypt 68, Cold Plains 63, Burial Grounds 48, Den 45, Dark Wood 36. Per minute spent: Cave Level 1 3.0 failures, Stony Field 1.5, so narrow geometry is worse (INFERRED: corridors and cave walls make the nearest walkable cell the player's own). The log has no player position and no monster kind on this line, so the exact cell geometry per event is UNKNOWN.
**What the bot does next (VERIFIED).** `Fighter.move` sleeps one `PollMs` (60 ms controller clock) and the back-off counter has already been incremented. Cost: 1,185 x 0.06 s = 71 s (0.1% of kill time, 0.13% of walk). It also reaches the 8-step cap sooner: 367 `back-off cap, melee` lines, 143 of them right after a failure. Share of the 70% walking explained: under 0.2% directly (INFERRED: the early melee has no measurable XP/h effect). Reading the soak report ("costs a re-plan each time", "monster behind a wall") is not what the code does: the target is the RETREAT point, not a monster, and no re-plan runs.
**Staleness check.** Not the position-jump staleness of the `Navigator.Sync` fix: `PathTo` and `GoTo` use the same grid and plan and agree on the plan; they disagree only on what an empty `wps` means.

It runs in town too and charges the whole gap to the kill that ends it, so a town trip lands in the next kill. ## 3. F1, the belt window and the `no healing potion in the belt` lines (463; soak 1: 92)
**The death (VERIFIED).** Log lines 21:09:40 to 21:09:45 (wall): trip with 495 gold after selling items (gold 90 before), bought 2 healing, 3 mana and 1 scroll, moved 5 potions to the belt (`hp 2->4 mp 1->4`), left with 155 gold, entered Burial Grounds (quest step `Level up to 5`, clvl 4, killed 14 skeletons and Hungry Dead for 34 to 44 XP each in 2 s wall). Life was 20 to 25 absolute (about 40 maximum) with 3 to 5 hostiles adjacent: 4 healing potions drunk in about 16 virtual s, belt healing 4, 3, 1, 0. At 34% with the belt empty `potion: not drinking because chose town-portal first`, then `d2d portal: click without a portal ... the cast was cut (source spent)`, then `no portal source (empty)`, death 22 ms wall later. Autopsy: killer Hungry Dead (mlvl 2, gap -2), last safety `crowd-retreat`, 1 scroll held, `no town portal cast`.
**Verdict.** The belt was full at entry, the scroll was held, the early-game spec "Belt before the field" was met. The avoidable parts: entering a Burial Grounds crowd at 40 maximum life (area choice, POLICY), and casting the portal at 34% in melee when a cast takes about 1.3 s (the abort rule `escape` returns PortalOut whenever a scroll exists; no rule checks that a cast under melee is cut). A chicken at 34% with 5 adjacent would have been the safer exit (INFERRED, UNVERIFIED how long a cut cast takes on d2d). `AbortFight` has no rule for "adjacent melee count versus cast time". Existing rules did not cover it: the chicken line is 25% with `lp > chicken-8` for PortalOut.
**The belt window (VERIFIED).** Windows with zero healing outside town (minute lines, belt only): 48 of 1,275 minutes (3.8%): clvl 2, minutes 12 to 21 (10 min, 53 `no healing` lines: the starting 4 potions used up, gold 25, `recovery: start (gold 0 < kit 500)`); the post-death window, minutes 61 to 87 (27 min, 217 lines of the 463, 156 `combat abort fight: flee`, 19 `no portal source`); the rest are 1 to 2 minute dips. So 58% of the 463 lines are two early episodes and soak 1 simply had no death. Post-death: gold 0 (death cost the carried gold, corpse worth 546 below the 1,000 threshold, gold sweep found 0), `poverty advice: play-lean ... nothing worth a sell trip` (sellable 1), the `[heal]` trip at 21:10:05 sold nothing (chores `equip`,`shop` only) with `sellable` 1 to 2, gold +16 in 25 virtual min (about 40 gold/h, Blood Moor already cleared in this game), until `sellable` reached 4 and the trip at 21:11:16 sold them for about 534 gold (4 items), bought 1 healing and 3 scrolls. So the window was ended by the item sale; a sell at the first heal trip would have shortened it (INFERRED, item values unknown).
**Purchases (VERIFIED).** The potion plan buys what the gold allows and the plan was always consistent with the gold at each visit (`broke: true` visits at 21:02:36 and 21:07:35). `belt: moved 0 potions`: 0 of 166, as the soak report says.
**Start of run (VERIFIED).** The starting belt (4 Minor Healing) was gone at minute 12 (clvl 2) and the first purchases were 1 potion at 31 gold: the kit of a fresh character is the cost model's problem (`gold 0 < kit 500`), not a policy bug.

## 4. F2 and F3: poverty loops and the no-kill stretches
**The economy (VERIFIED).** Gold spent in the run 35,235 (soak 1: 34,730): Scroll of Town Portal 184 = 18,345 (52%), Minor Mana Potion 231 = 13,860 (39%), Minor Healing 91 = 2,730 (8%; soak 1: 3,990), Tome 1. Income (positive minute deltas) 21.1k, of which sales in town about 11k and farming about 10k at 7 to 11 gold per virtual minute. Town trips: 174 (soak 1: 141); reasons `low on mana potions` 50 and `mp belt columns below N%` 37 (50% of trips), `no town portal scroll` 22, poverty/sell 24. At 10 gold/min one portal scroll (100 gold) is 10 minutes of farming and a mana potion (60) is 6, so the mana-driven trip loop (trip, scroll, potions, gold gone, farm again) is the poverty loop (INFERRED from VERIFIED numbers). `Recovery: farming gold` was 436 minutes (34%; soak 1 291 = 23%) and earned 52 XP/min at clvl 11 and above, the same as the quest step (60), so the loss is the low XP per kill of those areas, not idleness.
**Spec check (VERIFIED).** At clvl 11 and above the bot spent 69 minutes in Blood Moor (0.8 XP/kill), 115 in Cold Plains (4.4), 34 in Den (2.5), 61 in Burial Grounds (5.7) and 248 in Stony Field (16.9): 279 of 785 minutes (36%) in the areas the early-game spec says never to farm for XP, and the spec allows it for gold ("earns gold only"), which is exactly what recovery did. F2 as a window (22:26 to 22:41 wall) is one instance of this.
**F3, the 26 virtual lines (VERIFIED, 21:36:30 to 21:39:01 wall).** Step `Recovery: farming gold`, then `Quest: fighting (Level up to 17)` at clvl 10 in Stony Field (area 4): `roam: coverage points 1923`, zero kills for 21 virtual minutes (66,667 XP constant), then `task step level.17 blocked: explore: nav: timeout: roaming for 5m0s`, a retry, another 5 minutes. The 1,590 s gap in the killtime lines (a Skeleton, area 9 afterwards) is the end of this stretch plus the hops after it. Mechanism, in the order the log shows: (a) Stony Field has no monsters left in this game (INFERRED: "clear killed nothing" 1,344 times in the run, monsters respawn only in a fresh game); (b) the leveling roam keeps walking 1,923 coverage points for the 5-minute timeout; (c) a Den of Evil quest hop (`area skipped border-loop`, 12 bounces in 0.6 wall s, see section 5) and planner entries `area skipped unreachable` for Crypt, Cave 2, Black Marsh and Dark Wood in 20 ms (all `unreachable` at once, INFERRED: the route is blocked by the Den border-loop ban, `area ban lifted` fired for 6 areas in the same tick) leave Stony Field as the only candidate. So F3 is two things: policy on when to start a fresh game (the check `everything worth farming is cleared` fires too late) and a primitive: the roam does not know the area has no monsters left (it has no per-game kill memory), plus the ban handling in the planner (section 5). No paused lever (m2-roam, goldfloor) is the direct cause, but the fix for "when to recycle" is a policy change and stays paused.
**Other long gaps (VERIFIED).** Of the 25 longest gaps: 8 are `Recovery: farming gold` stretches in Blood Moor/Cold Plains with no kills for 5 to 17 virtual minutes (the 1,021 s one at clvl 4 is the post-death flee loop: 191 `no healing potion`, 149 flee), 9 are level hopping between Burial Grounds, Cave 1/2, Den, Blood Moor (20 to 100 `arrived in level` lines each), 5 are trips without scroll.

## 5. Border loop, level hopping and the Den of Evil bounce
VERIFIED: `area skipped ... border-loop` 110 (soak 1: 100), `unreachable` 108, `no eligible reachable leveling area for level 10/12` 38 blocked steps, `exit to Cold Plains: nav: the level changed during the walk: left level 2 for 3 but ended in 8` 25 blocked steps (the walk to the Cold Plains exit entered the Den of Evil entrance), 705 `nav: stepping away from a cave mouth`. At 21:37:47 (wall) the bot entered Den of Evil, the state flipped `traveling` to `clearing` to `traveling` and the bot was back in Blood Moor 12 times in 0.6 wall s (about 6 virtual s) until the border-loop ban (180 s) fired. The log shows 118 `quest.den_of_evil/to-den` steps in the run and the quest is a clvl 1 to 5 task, so the bot re-enters a Den that is probably done (INFERRED). INFERRED: the quest planner re-queues `quest.den_of_evil/to-den` when the leveling step is blocked; UNKNOWN why the quest is not marked done (read the quest state before deciding).

## 6. F5: waypoint fallbacks, walk-backs and the empty portal source
VERIFIED: 53 `no town portal: taking the waypoint to town` (soak 1: 33), 47 `no town portal and no waypoint: walking back via the neighbour`, 180 `d2d town portal: no portal source (empty)`, 22 trips with reason `no town portal scroll` costing 5,364 s (7% of kill time), 61 blocked leveling steps `town: no town portal available`. An "empty portal source" is the Portal primitive's answer when the pack has no scroll and the tome is empty (drill D2.b: no key, no click, error at tick 0, internal/combat/portal.go): it is a cheap refusal, not a failure, and the 180 lines are mostly the recovery step probing before it walks (59 follow `recovery: leaving Cave Level N first`, 28 `Den`/`Dark Wood`, 56 follow a `state` line). Cause: scrolls run out because the bot buys one per trip (184 in total, 100 gold each) and a Tome of Town Portal was bought once (300 gold) while the scroll count stays the unit; gold is the limit (section 4). The 53 fallbacks cluster in the poverty episodes (INFERRED from the minute lines: they follow `Chores in town` with 0 to 1 scroll). Same root as F1/F2, as the soak report says, with one correction: the portal itself works (98 arrivals through a portal; `no portal opened` 7, `portal did not lead` 7).

## 7. F6: `walkingRaw` (settled)
Code: `d2dinput.Controller` stores the raw `walking=` token of the last `state` read (internal/d2dinput/controller.go, `walkRaw`); `RunInfo`/`LogKV` print it in the minute line (internal/d2dinput/run.go). VERIFIED live (docs/probes/drills/README.md, DRILLS run lane): `walking=` is `0` while standing and `1` in a state read while moving, in BOTH running and walking, so it means "moving". In the soak the sample is the last read before the minute line, so 84.3% "1" is the share of minutes in which the bot was moving at the sample, which is consistent with the 70% walk bucket and says nothing about the run toggle. The run proof is stamina (drain means running) and `runBelieved` (84.7% of minutes) and the live 0.5628 vs 0.3753 subtiles per tick in the same README. No new probe is needed. A `debug players` read would add nothing, because `pmode` is -1 both ways (docs/d2d-run.md); the drill already used the `state` and `key r` verbs and stamina.

## 8. Gate criteria that depend on the unit of "minute" (not decided here)
Belt window (27 virtual lines = 87 s wall) and no-progress (26 virtual lines = 2.5 wall min). Both are the post-death/Stony Field stretches above. VERIFIED: soak 1 read them in virtual minutes. This is the user's decision (soak-gates spec); this study only notes that with the post-death window shortened (card 6 below) and the exhausted-area roam fixed (card 4) both would pass in virtual minutes too.
