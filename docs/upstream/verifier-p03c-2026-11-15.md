## d2d side, read (V read, r2)

- The player enters GH in exactly two places, both in `hurt_side`: the knock block (sets `knock`) and the big-hit block (resets `knock`), `tick.cpp:681` and `:738` (grep of `kModeGH` over `components/`). So "GH with `knock` unset" is exactly a real get-hit; Leap's landing knocks monsters back (`land(..., blow.knockback)`), never the player; the merc's knock is `knock_back(*actor->merc, ...)`, a monster path. Nothing else reaches GH with `knock` unset.
- A refused knock in GH moves nothing: the knock block is skipped whole (no `knock`, no `set_pmode`, no `pmode_until`); the slide code above it only moves the player when `knock` is set; the big-hit block refuses on `get_hit_running`; the struck-cue branch requires `pmode != GH`. The claim's unit assert checks hold, `knock` and `x`.
- **Knock and big hit in one call.** `Foe` aggregates every hit on the player in one tick. From GH, the knock is refused and `get_hit_running` refuses the get-hit: nothing changes, as game.exe's flag chain does for a knocking hit (refused, return) and for a big hit (refused). Right for this cell, but the unit test only uses a 1-point knock, so this case is not pinned (finding 4). From other modes the one-call order question (game.exe processes the two hits one after the other) is unchanged by the claim and outside it.
- **Knock with no room.** The claim's cell refuses before room matters, in both programs (`0x580adc` precedes the mode function, which builds the path; d2d's new condition precedes `unit_blocked`). Not relevant to the claim. Elsewhere (NU, no room) d2d starts no knock and then may give a get-hit; game.exe returns after flag 8. Pre-existing, U, outside the claim; the test's `World` has no level, so no-room is never exercised.
