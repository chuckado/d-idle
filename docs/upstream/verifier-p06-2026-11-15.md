- `FUN_005ad730`: `0x5ad7f9 cmp [esi+8], edx(0); 0x5ad7fc setne dl; push edx; push 1; push ebx; call 0x57dfb0` at `0x5ad806`: can-block is `record + 8 != 0`.

- `FUN_005a89a0`: `+8` = 0 unless stat 0x16 > 0 and stat 0x15 > 0 (`0x5a89c9..0x5a89e2`), else a roll in min..max (`FUN_0045c3e0` on the missile's seed `+0x20`), stored at `0x5a8a06`. Later in the same function (`0x5a8be3..0x5a8bff`) `+8 += +8 x pct / 100` with pct clamped to at least -90, and `0x5a8c1e` may double it: both keep 0 at 0 and a positive value positive, so can-block = physical min and max both over 0. The oracle's call passes 0 as the second argument and skips that branch; this does not change the sign (V read).
- Callers: `FUN_005a89a0` at `0x5ade7d`, then the record straight to `0x5adcd0 -> FUN_005ad730` (`0x5adcd5`), nothing between. A second caller of FUN_005ad730 at `0x57e203` copies a caller's record (finding 7).
