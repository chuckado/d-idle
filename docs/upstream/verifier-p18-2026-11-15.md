## Required changes

0. **Seed-dependent `test_combat` (high, CI).** Widen lines 511 and 515 of `tests/test_combat.cpp` in the claim commit (done in r3):
   `through.damage >= 12 && through.damage <= 13` (10..11 points at +20 %: 12 to 13.2) and `holy_shield.damage >= (10 + 4) * 150 / 100 && holy_shield.damage <= (10 + 4 + 1) * 150 / 100` (21 to 22.5). The body's sentence "it now allows 15 or 16" should name all four asserts.

1. **No `## Checklist` and no finding in `docs/research/re/` (medium, process).** The upstream template asks for both. `docs/research/re/skills.md`'s Smite bullet ("into FUN_0057b420 on the shield with ED = calc1, SrcDam 128") says nothing of stat 111 or the width. Add one sentence to it in the claim commit, e.g. "FUN_0057b420 on that path (EDX 0) adds stat 111 << 8 to both ends, lifts a minimum under 1 to 0x100 and a maximum not above the minimum to minimum + 0x100 (0x57b4c3..0x57b4f4), before the percents". Then add the Checklist with three ticked boxes (`test_smite_damage`, `skills.md`, `pr_guard` 0/0). P17's verifier asked for the same.

2. **Stats 17 and 18 are one axis (medium, Limits).** game.exe takes the two ends' percents separately: `[ebp+0x14]` = stat 0x12 (18, min %) is added to the minimum's percent and `[ebp-4]` = stat 0x11 (17, max %) to the maximum's (`0x57b504..0x57b5d5`, V read). d2d's `smite_pct` uses stat 17 for both ends. The grid always sets 17 = 18 (`gear_pct`), so it cannot see this. In vanilla items the `dmg%` property sets both together (I), so the claim is not wrong, but the body's "gear stats 17 and 18 {0, 15, 40}" reads as two axes. Write in Limits: "stats 17 and 18 are set equal in every cell; game.exe applies 18 to the minimum and 17 to the maximum, d2d 17 to both; not compared".

3. **Reach wording (low).** The four stat 111 items are found through the `dmg` property (`Properties.txt`: `dmg` = func 1, stat `item_normaldamage`, D). They are Stone Crusher (`dmg` 10..30), Ironward (40..85), The Redeemer (60..120) and the runeword Widowmaker (340..400) (D). The three uniques are one-hand maces and a scepter, so a Paladin can hold one with a shield, and their stat 111 is what Smite now adds. Widowmaker is a bow and can never be held with a shield, so it is no Smite reach. Say "the weapon's stat 111 counts (the unit's stats, as the plain swing)". The Zakarum Shield is indeed the only `armor.txt` row with `mindam == maxdam` (46/46, D).

## Game side, re-derived (V read)

- **The call, FUN_005ce9f0 `0x5cea5d..0x5ceb31`:** player only (`cmp [esi], 0`). FUN_0063c8f0 finds the shield (`[ebp-0x18]`), FUN_006335f0 its record, and min = byte `+0xfe` << 8, max = byte `+0xff` << 8. While state 0x65 (Holy Shield, FUN_00639df0) is on, FUN_00647bc0 / FUN_00647d00 of its stat list are added. Then FUN_0057b420(ecx = unit, edx = 0, item, min, max, ED = FUN_00646ca0(skill +0x138), 0, 0x80). That is exactly the oracle's call (`smite_damage.py` `e.call(0x57b420, item, min, max, ed, 0, 0x80, edx=0)`).
- **FUN_0057b420 with `edx = 0`:** jumps straight to `0x57b4c3`, skipping the weapon's 0x15..0x18 reads. Then `FUN_00625500(unit, 0x6f, 0)` << 8 is added to max and min (`0x57b4c3..0x57b4d6`); `cmp ecx, 1; jge` else min = 0x100 (`0x57b4d8..0x57b4e0`); `cmp [max], min; jg` else max = min + 0x100 (`0x57b4ea..0x57b4f4`). The claim's `base_low < 1 → 256` and `base_high <= base_low → base_low + 256` are the same tests in the same order, after Holy Shield's term, which is already inside min and max. Confirmed.
- **Which stat 111:** FUN_00625500 is FUN_00625420 on the unit's list `+0x5c`, and FUN_00625480 is the same list through the same reader FUN_00624f60, which reads the full list `+0x48` of an extended list. So Smite reads the unit's summed stat 111, as the plain swing does: d2d's `sum`, not the shield's own stats, is right. FUN_00624f60's only extra is a minimum clamp for rows with flag `+5 & [0x6ce270]` (= 4). Stat 111's row has the flag clear and minimum 0 (V run, booted tables: `isc.py`, which also gives Str and Dex minimum 1, flag set; the grid's 30 / 100 are above it). So the oracle's plain stat hook hides no clamp, and the -30 cells are valid inputs (no item makes stat 111 negative, D).
- **Percents:** ebx = ED + stat 25 + Str × item Str bonus / 100 + Dex × item Dex bonus / 100 + FUN_00645830 (mastery; 0 for a shield, I: Paladins have none), floored at -90 (`cmp ebx, -0x5a`); low = min + MulDiv(min, stat 18 + ebx, 100), high = MulDiv(max, stat 17 + ebx, 100) + max; a roll FUN_0045c3e0 on `unit + 0x20` when high > low. For non-negative percents `min + min × p / 100` equals d2d's `min × (100 + p) / 100`. The -90 floor applies to ED included in game.exe, but d2d floors before adding ED: that is the trace's D3/D5, not claimed and not reached by the grid (no negative percent).
- **Draw order:** in FUN_005ce9f0 the damage roll (`0x5ceb31`) comes before the hit roll FUN_0057ec10 (`0x5ceba8`), so the damage roll is the first draw of the Smite and the per-seed compare is meaningful for play too (the earlier FUN_00553540 at `0x5cea2f` was not read: U whether it draws).

Do the hooks hide a rule? No (V read). The stat getters stand in for the unit's stats 0, 2, 17, 18, 25, 111 (no clamp, above); FUN_00629860 / FUN_006298a0 return the shield's bonuses; FUN_00645830 returns 0 (no Paladin mastery); FUN_00535bc0 / FUN_0063d340 are on the `edx != 0` path only, so they are never reached here. The hidden split of stats 17 and 18 is required change 2.

## Is the test_combat change legitimate?

Yes, for the two asserts it changed: a 10..10 shield at +50 % now rolls 2,560..2,816 × 1.5 = 15 to 16.5 points, which is game.exe's width rule (`0x57b4ea`), not masking. The old exact 15 encoded the deviation. The block tests still tell 0 from 15/16, so they keep their purpose. The two asserts it did not change are required change 0.

## Brief question 4: is a rule this rare worth a PR?

Yes, with the reach said plainly. It is a two-line rule with an exact differential (63,709 cells to 0), both terms proven necessary by mutants, and no effect outside Smite. Reach in play: a Paladin wielding Stone Crusher, Ironward or The Redeemer gets +10..120 Smite damage per hit that d2d dropped, and every Zakarum Shield Smite gets its one point of width. Those are not negligible for those players. The weakness is the test churn (required change 0), not the rule.
