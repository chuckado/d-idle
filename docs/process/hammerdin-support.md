# Paladin Hammerdin: what the engine plays, and what a later card must build

> status: current (card hammer1, 2026-11-20; Fable roadmap Q8, class 3 after the Sorceress fire build and the Necromancer summoner). Design note, no combat code. Evidence tags: V verified (how said), S read in code or docs, U unverified. Sources: `internal/combat/classdata/profiles.json` and `skills.json`, `internal/combat/fight.go`, `docs/classes/paladin.md` (Maxroll guides, Kolbot notes, V there), `docs/design/fable-review-2026-11-19/03-classes-plan.md` section 3.3 (row P2), `data/capabilities/capabilities.json`.

## 1. Finding: the default Paladin already reaches for the hammer

The default Paladin profile has a band from clvl 18 that attacks with Blessed Hammer first (`requires: Blessed Hammer`, aura ladder Prayer at rest, Concentration, Holy Fire in a crowd, Might), and `skills.json` types it `ground`, range 9, left hand, `hold` (S). The shipped endgame table `paladin_hammerdin_endgame90` is the Hell track of the same idea. What was missing is a Normal-to-Hell track a player can pick that does not spend points on Zeal and Sacrifice, and a profile that does not play them. Nothing of the hammer loop has a live verdict (U): the capability rows say so.

## 2. Capability rows (audited, none changed)

All 30 Paladin rows exist (supported Zeal, Might, Holy Fire; experimental 23; unsupported Charge, Vengeance, Conversion, Fist of the Heavens). Audit of the rows the Hammerdin touches, S from the table and V where `internal/agency` overlays skills.txt: Blessed Hammer (ground, mag, reqLevel 18, needs Holy Bolt, synergy Concentration), Concentration (needs Blessed Aim), Vigor (needs Cleansing and Defiance), Holy Shield (needs Charge and Blessed Hammer). No evidence in hand says a tier or a requirement is wrong (the table's own plan expansion and `buildfile.CheckTable` accept the guide table row by row), so no row changed and no must-fail was added. One point for a later card: the brief of this card named Vigor as a Blessed Hammer synergy; the table lists only Concentration (U, skills.txt not readable on the head box; check `DmgSymPerCalc` of Blessed Hammer on the worker host).

## 3. The build: `paladin_hammerdin_guide` (experimental, opt-in)

- File of record: `internal/buildfile/testdata/paladin-hammerdin-guide.json` (`didle.build/1`); the plan table `internal/plandata/build-paladin_hammerdin_guide.json` (copy in `docs/plan/data/`) is its expansion (`TestPaladinHammerdinGoalFileMatchesTable`, `UPDATE=1` rewrites the rows) and passes `buildfile.CheckTable` under the capability rules.
- Track (no respec, S: review 03 section 3.3 guide track): Might 1 and Smite 6 carry clvl 2 to 17 (Smite is the one-shield melee skill the Paladin owns), Holy Bolt 1 is the hammer's prerequisite, every point from 18 goes to Blessed Hammer with Concentration beside it (Blessed Aim first), Holy Shield 1 at 24 (pulls Charge in), then Concentration, Vigor (pulls Prayer, Cleansing, Defiance in) and both to 20. The community track respecs at 19 (Holy Fire to 24); a respec is not in this file. At clvl 30 the table holds Might 1, Smite 6, Holy Bolt 1, Blessed Hammer 14, Blessed Aim 1, Concentration 8, Charge 1, Holy Shield 1 (V `TestPaladinHammerdinBuildSelectableAndDefaultsUnchanged`). Points land late for Vigor (well after 30) by the table's own arithmetic. Stats Str 60 (gear), Dex 70 (block), rest Vitality, no Energy (U, from the endgame table).
- Selector and combat line: the table's data (`selector`, `combat: hammerdin`, `tier: experimental`). Run it with `levelerBuild paladin_hammerdin_guide`. The default Paladin (`paladin-normal30`, endgame `paladin_hammerdin_endgame90`) is unchanged (V the same test, and the Necromancer and Sorceress defaults beside it).
- Profile `paladin/hammerdin` (`profiles.json`, data only): Smite then the plain attack before 18, Blessed Hammer then Smite from 18; auras Prayer at rest, Concentration in the hammer band, Might; Holy Shield as the buff; layout F1 Smite, F2 Blessed Hammer, F3 Holy Shield, F4 Concentration, F5 Vigor, F6 Might, F7 Prayer, F8 Thorns; no Zeal or Sacrifice, no life price; the `melee` block is the default Paladin's, copied, not tuned (U). Unknown Paladin profile or another class asking for `hammerdin` is an error naming it (V `TestPaladinHammerdinProfile`).
- Planner shadow (`agency.planner` knob, shadow only): sane at every clvl 1 to 30 for this table and for `paladin-normal30-v2` beside it; from clvl 18 the plan's attack is Blessed Hammer and agrees with the profile at 18, 24 and 30; at 6 and 12 the plan says Attack and the profile Smite (Smite is a 0 dps row in the estimator, documented in `testdata/agreement.json`); clvl 30 raises `unsupported_points` (the Charge prerequisite point) and `mana_starved`, as a Hammerdin without Insight should (V `internal/agency` tests).

## 4. What a later card must build (nothing of this is implemented here)

| id | need | evidence | size |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Hammer aiming: Blessed Hammer is cast at a point in front and the hammers spiral outward; Kolbot casts only at a target within 9 and picks a free ground cell next to the hero (V docs/classes/paladin.md "Attack"). Today `KindGround` aims at the monster's tile with range 9 and `hold`; whether that spawns a useful spiral against a pack, a wall or a single boss is U. Needs a live look at where the hammers land and the hit rate | classdata, docs/classes/paladin.md | medium, live |
| H2 | Concentration while casting: the aura ladder picks Prayer at rest and Concentration in the band, but a switch costs about 5 frames and a dropped aura ends the damage bonus; a re-assert rule keyed on the aura state read back (the Might path has V live in `aura.go`, Concentration has none) | docs/classes/paladin.md section on auras | small, live |
| H3 | Mana: a hammer costs mana that scales with level and a Hammerdin has no Insight (no ladder words, `docs/design/fable-review-2026-11-19/03` K11/Q4): mana potions in the belt, Vigor and Prayer as sustain, a rest-until-mana rule, energy gear. The planner already flags `mana_starved` at clvl 30; the engine only has `manaReserve` hysteresis | profile, planner warnings | medium |
| H4 | Vigor as a walking aura: it is only useful while moving (speed, stamina, life); no band lists it and the aura ladder has no "while walking" slot, so it stays a layout key | profiles.json | small |
| H5 | Positioning: hammers have a blind spot at walls and corridors (Maggot Lair), the hero should stand in open ground inside the pack and step out when surrounded; the default melee block (`surround`, `retreatLifePct`) is a Zealot's | docs/classes/paladin.md "Dangers" | medium |
| H6 | Holy Shield recast on expiry (the buff slot casts it by state; the state number is U), and a block-aware stat rule for Dex | profile buffs | small |
| H7 | Charge: Holy Shield needs it as a prerequisite point only; the capability row stays unsupported (a charge move is not an attack loop) | capabilities.json | none |
| H8 | Teleport-less pathing: the Hammerdin walks. The walker, not the profile, owns this; Enigma is not on the list for the idle hero | review 03 | not ours |
| H9 | Respec: the community track respecs at 19; the no-respec track here banks about 10 points at clvl 17 (Holy Bolt waits for 6, the hammer for 18) and plays Smite to 17. A respec goal for the buildfile (`respec_at`) is supported by the format, not wired into this table | buildfile README | small |
