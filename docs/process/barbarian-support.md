# Barbarian Frenzy: what the engine plays, and what a later card must build

> status: current (card barb1, 2026-11-20; Fable roadmap Q8, class 4 after the Sorceress fire build, the Necromancer summoner and the Hammerdin). Design note, no combat code. Evidence tags: V verified (how said), S read in code or docs, U unverified. Sources: `internal/combat/fight.go`, `rules.go`, `gearcheck.go`, `classdata/profiles.json` and `skills.json`, `docs/classes/barbarian.md` (Maxroll guides, Kolbot notes, live results of agent BARBLIVE, V there), `docs/design/fable-review-2026-11-19/03-classes-plan.md` sections 2 and 3.4, the patched skills.txt of the worker host (requirements, mana columns, srvdofunc: V).

## 1. Finding: which build, and what exists

The review names B1 Frenzy as the first Barbarian (roadmap row 4: "Frenzy, then Concentrate"; reuses the Paladin melee driver) and says of B2 Whirlwind "needs the movement-attack driver ... do not start here". The brief of this card said `barbarian_whirlwind_guide` unless the review names another; it does, so the build is `barbarian_frenzy_guide`. Whirlwind stays an unsupported row.

The default Barbarian already plays a Frenzy line: `barbarian_melee_normal30` (Bash, Double Swing, Frenzy from 24, V live to clvl 5 only) and profile `barbarian` with Frenzy from clvl 18. What was missing: a table a player can pick on purpose that is written as a build file, a profile that does not spend a buff slot on a passive, and the capability rows for the other 14 skills. Nothing of Frenzy has a live verdict (U): no dual-wield Frenzy was ever seen.

## 2. Capability rows (30, `data/capabilities/capabilities.json`)

| tier | n | skills |
|---|---|---|
| supported | 10 | passives with nothing to cast: Sword, Axe, Mace, Pole Arm, Throwing and Spear Mastery, Increased Stamina, Increased Speed, Natural Resistance, Iron Skin |
| experimental | 10 | Bash, Double Swing, Frenzy, Stun, Concentrate, Berserk (melee through the generic path, `skills.json` rows with hand left; only the first three are on a band), Howl, Taunt, Shout, Battle Orders (cast through the buff ladder, never seen live) |
| unsupported | 10 | Whirlwind, Leap, Leap Attack (movement while attacking, kind K12), War Cry, Battle Cry (stun and curse novas, no loop, no positioning), Battle Command (a layout key but in no buff ladder), Double Throw (throwing, K15), Find Potion, Find Item, Grim Ward (corpse targets and a weapon swap) |

Every row has kind, delivery, tier with a reason, element for damage rows, `reqLevel` and `reqSkills` (V, read from the patched skills.txt, charclass `bar`), synergies and the mana source. Findings from reading it:
- Iron Skin is a passive in skills.txt (no `srvdofunc`, `passivestate ironskin`: V). The old row said buff, experimental, and the default profile lists it in its buff ladder; a cast of a passive does nothing. The row is corrected to passive and the new profile leaves it out. The default profile is not changed here (a later card removes the entry; U that it costs anything today).
- Frenzy requires Double Throw (not Double Swing), Double Throw requires Double Swing, Berserk requires Concentrate requires Stun requires Bash, Whirlwind requires Leap Attack and Concentrate, War Cry requires Battle Cry and Battle Orders (V skills.txt). The shipped tables and the new one are legal under these rules (`TestShippedTablesAreLegal` now covers a fourth class).
- Barbarian synergies: skills.txt has a damage synergy (`DmgSymPerCalc`) only for War Cry (Howl, Taunt, Battle Cry). The `calc` references of Frenzy, Concentrate and Berserk (Double Swing, Taunt, Bash, Battle Orders, Howl, Shout) are defence and speed bonuses, not damage, so those rows have none (U that no formula of `power` needs them). Shout, Battle Orders and Battle Command raise each other's duration.
- Mana: all Barbarian skills are cheap (Bash 2, Double Swing 8 falling by 1 per level, Frenzy 3, Berserk 4, Whirlwind 25 +1 per level, War Cry 10 +1 per level, Battle Orders 7: V skills.txt) but the pool is 10 at clvl 1 and grows by 1 energy per level (U: about 35 at clvl 30), so the Barbarian has mana and uses it every swing; there is no rage. Rest-until-mana (`manaReserve` 4) is the only management (S).

## 3. The build: `barbarian_frenzy_guide` (experimental, opt-in)

- File of record: `internal/buildfile/testdata/barbarian-frenzy-guide.json` (`didle.build/1`); the plan table `internal/plandata/build-barbarian_frenzy_guide.json` (copy in `docs/plan/data/`) is its expansion, kept in step by `TestBarbarianFrenzyGoalFileMatchesTable` (`UPDATE=1`), and passes `buildfile.CheckTable`.
- Track (no respec, S: review 03 B1 and SoloPlay's Double Swing then Frenzy at 24): Bash 1, Axe Mastery 3 (the start weapon is a hand axe, V live BARBLIVE), Double Swing from clvl 6 to 13 (Howl and Shout 1 each, the Battle Orders prerequisites), Double Throw 1 and Increased Stamina 2 at 12, Iron Skin 3 at 18, Frenzy from clvl 24, one Battle Orders; later Berserk 1 (Stun and Concentrate are its prerequisite points, clvl 38), Natural Resistance, Battle Orders 10, Frenzy and Iron Skin 20. At clvl 30 the table holds Bash 1, Axe Mastery 3, Double Swing 13, Howl 1, Shout 1, Double Throw 1, Increased Stamina 2, Iron Skin 3, Frenzy 7, Battle Orders 1 (V `TestBarbarianFrenzyBuildSelectableAndDefaultsUnchanged`). Stats Str 60 at 2 per level, Dex 40 at 1, rest Vitality (U numbers; the class starts at 30 20 25 10).
- Selector and combat line: the table's data (`selector`, `combat: frenzy`, `tier: experimental`). Run it with `levelerBuild barbarian_frenzy_guide`. The default Barbarian (`barbarian_melee_normal30`, endgame `barbarian_warcry_endgame90`) and the Paladin, Necromancer and Sorceress defaults are unchanged (V the same test).
- Profile `barbarian/frenzy` (`profiles.json`, data only): Bash then plain attack before clvl 6, Double Swing from 6, Frenzy first from 24 (`requires: Frenzy`); buffs Battle Orders then Shout; layout F1 Bash, F2 Double Swing, F3 Frenzy, F4 Battle Orders, F5 Shout, F6 Howl, F7 Berserk, F8 Concentrate; the `melee` block is the default Barbarian's, copied, not tuned (U). Unknown Barbarian profile or another class asking for `frenzy` is an error naming it (V `TestBarbarianFrenzyProfile`).
- Planner shadow (`agency.planner` knob, shadow only): sane at every clvl 1 to 30 for this table and for `barbarian_melee_normal30` beside it, no unsupported skill in any slot; the plan and the profile agree on the attack at all five checkpoints 6, 12, 18, 24, 30 (Double Swing, then Frenzy at 30; V `TestBarbarianFrenzyPlanAndProfile`, `testdata/agreement.json`).
