## Limits of this measure

- The denominators are only what the data tables list (rows, plus the hand-listed rule set under `formulas`). Code paths outside the tables (movement, pathing, packets, timing, the server loop) are not counted at all; the number says nothing about them.
- A row's status is the best evidence on any one aspect of it, named in the ledger note. A skill marked run-tested for its missile pipeline can still be untested for its mana or its states; the cross-cutting `skillfx.*` subsystems exist to show those aspects separately.
- Untested means unknown, not wrong. Run-tested with 0 diff means 0 differences on the cases generated, not a proof; the case count and seed are in the ledger.
- READ-ONLY rows were read in game.exe by an agent and are not offered as proof of anything.
- The ledger is hand-maintained. The generator checks that every cited document exists, that a V run claim has a case count that appears in the cited document, and that rows are not claimed twice; it cannot check that the document is right.
- Denominators were read from the patched 1.14d tables of the worker host's install; `--tables DIR` re-checks them against any local extracted cache.
