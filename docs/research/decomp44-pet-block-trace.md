# decomp44: does a player's pet block (golem, raised, summon)? game.exe against d2d

> status: current, 2026-11-20 (agent decomp44, Sonnet; docs plus scripts in the agent's own scratch; nothing posted, no live game, no PR candidate). Follows [decomp43-monster-block-verify.md](decomp43-monster-block-verify.md) section 7 (its inferred deviation D6, "d2d pets never block") and [decomp43-monster-block-trace.md](decomp43-monster-block-trace.md). The monster block gate itself is the claim of `fid/monster-block-r2` (see [../upstream/bodies/monster-block-body.md](../upstream/bodies/monster-block-body.md)).

Tags: **V read** (game.exe disassembly or d2d source read here), **V run** (a game.exe value that the existing oracle already ran natively, read from its stored output), **I** (inferred), **U** (unverified). Nothing new was run in game.exe for this doc: the block value is the monster-block oracle's, the pet path was read.

## 0. Answer in short

A pet blocks in game.exe by the same function as any monster (`FUN_00622720` on the pet unit, so gate A `NoShldBlock` or a layer-7 shield on the pet's look). d2d gives a pet no block at all (V read). Of the player's pets only the **Necro-Skeleton** (ToBlock 3 on every difficulty, 4 of its 5 look entries carry a shield) and a **revived monster that carries a shield** can block; every golem, the Valkyrie, the Decoy, the Shadows, the Hydras, the Druid's summons and the Necro-Mage have chance 0 in game.exe (V run, stored `game.tsv`). **No PR candidate**: the claim cannot be proven with the existing differential, because the pet's look (which entry of the shield list it carries) is also wrong in d2d (a fixed first entry, never a shield) and no oracle yet runs game.exe's pet creation to compare the look; fixing the block alone changes nothing a player can see. What blocks, and the drill and oracle plan, in sections 3 to 5. Effect on play is small (3 percent of the blows at most on a Necro-Skeleton, 9 to 30 percent on a revived shield carrier).

## 1. Questions

- **Q1. Which function decides whether a struck unit blocks, and is it specific to the unit's owner?** V read. `FUN_0057dfb0` (`0x57dfb0..0x57e08b`): with can-block 0 it goes straight to `FUN_0057dd60` (dodge and avoid); else it calls `FUN_00622720(defender, [attacker+0x70])` (`0x57dfd5..0x57dfdb`) and a chance of 0 or less also goes to `FUN_0057dd60`. The chance is divided by 3 only for a defender of unit type 0 (a player) who passes the moving test `FUN_00622d00` (`0x57dffc..0x57e01f`: `cmp [esi], 0`); then the roll on the defender's own seed (`0x57e021..0x57e067`). Nothing looks at an owner: a pet is a unit of type 1 and takes `FUN_00622720`'s monster branch (gate A, gate B), identical to a hostile monster (decomp43 Q1). The three call sites of `FUN_0057dfb0` (melee hit entry `0x57ece8`, missile hit `0x5ad806`, `0x56ba0d`) are decomp27 Q1 (S); a monster's melee blow on a pet and a monster's missile on a pet both reach it.
- **Q2. Which player pets have a block chance in game.exe, by row?** V run (the stored game side of `monster_block.py`, `/data` case file of decomp43, 2,469 cases; every row, every difficulty, every shield-list entry): `claygolem`, `bloodgolem`, `irongolem`, `firegolem`, `valkyrie`, `dopplezon` (Decoy), `shadowwarrior`, `shadowmaster` (ToBlock 50 in the table, but no shield list and no `NoShldBlock`: 0), `hydra1..3`, `druidhawk`, `spiritwolf`, `spiritofbarbs`, `vinecreature`, `druidbear`, `wolf`, `necromage`: **0 at every entry and difficulty**. `necroskeleton`: entry 0 (`nil`) 0; entries 1 to 4 (the four shield codes) **3** at Normal, Nightmare and Hell. Revive (do 58) makes a pet of any row that has a corpse: the shield carriers among them (the skeletons 1 to 8: 10 to 30; Fallen 1 to 8 and others: 9 to 49 by row; the table is the claim's `game.tsv`) block by their own look.
- **Q3. How does a pet get its look in game.exe?** V read. The pet spawn `FUN_005b2f20` calls the monster spawner `FUN_005b2a00` (`0x5b2f5e`, `0x5b3001`..), which calls the unit creator `FUN_00555230` (`0x5b2e3d`); that reaches `FUN_00574250` (`0x5553a0`) and `FUN_00573cb0` (`0x5742e4`), whose first call is the look pick `FUN_005739d0` (`0x573d43`): per layer a roll on the unit's own seed over `FUN_006647c0(unit, layer)` entries, or a copy of a region component set when the third argument (`FUN_00547bc0`'s result, `0x5742dc`) is not null. So a pet's look is **rolled** like any monster's, and its layer-7 byte (`+0xb`) decides gate B. Whether `FUN_00547bc0` returns a set for a pet's class: not read (U).
- **Q4. Does Revive give the new pet a new look?** I. `FUN_005c56c0` (do 58, `0x5c56c0..`) calls neither `FUN_00555230` nor `FUN_00574250` nor `FUN_005739d0` (its call list, V read, `0x5c5728..0x5c5a15`: the unit lookup `FUN_00553540`, stat setters `FUN_00625480`/`FUN_00627260`, `FUN_005c4470`, `FUN_005c55c0`); the sub-calls `FUN_005c5430`, `FUN_005c55c0`, `FUN_005c4470` were not followed. d2d's own comment says Revive raises the corpse itself (`summons.cpp` just above the `raised` branch). So the revived unit keeps the corpse's `+0xb`. Not run (U).
- **Q5. What does d2d do?** V read, `8ae5cfcb`/`c1b38311` trees. `Fight::pet_foe` (`components/game/fight/pets.cpp:81..93`) builds the pet's `Fighter` with `simple_fighter(min, max, to_hit, defense)`; `Fighter::block` is its default 0 (`components/rules/combat.hpp:66`), so `monster_blow` never rolls a block on a pet (`components/rules/combat.cpp:724`). `summon_one` (`components/game/fight/summons.cpp:208`) sets every layer's look to `parts[layer].front()` (the first entry: `nil` for the Necro-Skeleton), not a roll on the pet's seed, and a revived pet gets the front entry too (it copies the corpse's stats, props and seed, `:218..224`, not its `npc.comp`).
- **Q6. Is the pet's look a port?** I. `spawn_monsters` rolls the look from the unit seed (`monster_look`, `ai.cpp:210..215`), the population port's claim (S: commit `43a2bcc5`). A pet is not made through it, so its look is never the rolled one in d2d.

## 3. What blocks a candidate

1. **Not provable with the existing differential.** The block value per row and entry is proven (`monster_block.py game`, V run), but the pet's `Fighter` is built outside that harness. A claim "pets block" in `pet_foe` alone changes nothing: the Necro-Skeleton's look is `nil` (never a shield), and a revived Fallen's `comp[7]` is its type's front entry, not its corpse's. A change that has an effect needs D6b or D6c as well, whose oracles (a native pet creation or revive) do not exist.
2. **Seed link (D6b).** For the pet look to follow the unit seed, d2d's pet seed must be game.exe's per pet; the population port proves that for spawned monsters (S), not for summons.
3. **Reach is small** (I): the Necro-Skeleton's chance is 3 percent of the blows a monster lands on it, and revive carriers are a fraction of all revives. A Necromancer idle bot would be the one to notice, and it has no pet-survival model (section 6).

Effort to a candidate, by the order of value: (1) D6c in the claim: copy the corpse's `npc.comp` in `summon_one(raised)`, test as above (S-size, but the revive oracle is needed to prove the corpse keeps `+0xb`: a native `FUN_005c56c0` run on a spawned unit, M); (2) D6a: one line in `pet_foe`, `fighter.block = pet.monster.block_chance(*game_data)` (the `block_chance` of the monster-block branch, which does not exist upstream until that PR merges: build `fid/pet-block-r1` on `fid/monster-block-r2`'s merge, not before); (3) D6b: after the seed link is read.

## 4. Drill recipe (lab binary; not run here)

K1 (D6a, D6b): lab binary of docs/d2d-fork.md, a throwaway Necromancer, Raise Skeleton at a corpse in Act 1 Normal; `debug nearby` or the `monsters` verb for the pet's unit id; have a Fallen hit it 200 times (`debug spawn fallen1` near it): expect 0 blocks in the lab (d2d), 3 percent of the blows in game.exe for a skeleton with a shield look. Compare the visible look: raise 20 skeletons, count shields (game.exe about 4 of 5; d2d none). K2 (D6c): raise a Fallen that has a shield (`debug spawn fallen1` until one shows a shield layer, kill it, Revive it); expect its look unchanged in game.exe, front entry in d2d.

## 6. Bot findings (described, not changed)

- **B1.** The bot has no model of a pet's survival or a pet's block: the Necromancer code and tests (`internal/combat/necro_test.go`, `internal/d2/units.go`) treat the pet as a unit to count, not as a defender (U: grep only). A kill-time or pet-life model must use game.exe's rule (the two gates) when it is added; d2d's lack of a pet block makes its pets 3 percent more fragile than game.exe's (Necro-Skeleton) until D6a to D6c are fixed, so any d2d-measured pet survival is a little pessimistic for those pets.
