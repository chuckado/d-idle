# Paladin auras: how they work and what the bot does (2026-11-26)

> status: current SME answer to the owner question of 2026-11-26. Tags: V verified (repo doc or code, path given), S veteran memory (classic 1.14d), U unsure. The Arreat Summit paladin page was not reachable this turn (the fetch returned "Document Not Found"); web search returned only D2R forum reports, so mechanics below are S unless a repo path is named.

## Verdict
The owner is half right. An aura must be learned and then SELECTED and CAST once (hotkey or right skill) to switch on, and a game rejoin or death drops it. But it does NOT have to stay on the selected slot. The bot's design already matches the true mechanic and reads the real active aura from memory. The Paladin is not scuffed by the premise "auras are assumed on". It is weakened by four other things: the Prayer ladder, Sacrifice use, no respec, and the merc pick (section 4).

## 1. How an aura works in 1.14d
- Learned, not equipped (S): spend skill points (items add +skill levels only). Nothing is "equipped". An unlearned aura cannot be selected.
- Activation (S, V for the memory side): selecting the aura as the right skill by hotkey turns it on at once with no mana cost; V live: a hotkey switch shows in memory within 20 ms, no mana (internal/combat/aura.go header, CLASSPAL). With no aura active a right click casts it (code comment in keepAura). Via the skill popup the aura also comes up (V live, docs/palready.md: SetRight through the right popup, read back).
- Stays on after another skill is selected (S, U for the TP edge): an aura runs until another aura replaces it, you die, you leave the game, or you respec. Switching the right slot to a non-aura skill (Charge, Town Portal) or the left skill to Zeal does not stop it; this is why hammerdins keep Concentration while the left hand casts. A D2R forum report says casting a portal from the right slot dropped the aura, but players answered that using the portal from inventory or belt does not; the report is D2R, treat as U for 1.14d.
- Only ONE aura of your own at a time (S). A second aura of a different source stacks: the merc aura and a party member's aura are separate states (V docs/sme/mercenary.md: Desert merc shares one aura with the party).
- Area change and town portal: the aura state is a player state; it normally survives (S), but the repo treats area change and death as "dirty" and re-checks (V internal/combat/aura.go keepAura). Death: state cleared (S). Rejoin or new game: not running; the right skill slot is remembered but the aura must be re-cast (S, U: the saved right skill may re-arm it on load; the bot's check covers both).
- Paladin skills by kind (S): AURAS, all active (need one cast, then run): Might, Prayer, Resist Fire, Resist Cold, Resist Lightning, Defiance, Cleansing, Vigor, Holy Fire, Thorns, Holy Freeze, Salvation, Meditation, Redemption, Concentration, Sanctuary, Fanaticism, Conviction, Holy Shock. ATTACKS: Sacrifice, Zeal, Smite, Charge, Vengeance, Holy Bolt, Blessed Hammer, Fist of the Heavens. BUFF cast: Holy Shield. Passive skills: the Paladin has none; Blessed Aim, Vigor and the resist auras are active auras, not passives (the Amazon and Necromancer have passives, the Paladin does not).

## 2. What the bot assumes (V code)
- Reads the active aura: player StatList child, state stat 350 = aura skill id (internal/combat/aura.go ActiveAura, VERIFIED LIVE CLASSPAL docs/classes/smoke-paladin.md finding 6). If unreadable, falls back to the selected right skill, trusted only while not dirty (auraDirty on area change or death).
- Applies it: on d2d with the right skill popup (SetRight, V live docs/palready.md: hotkey casts and `cmd select` did not change rskill); aura retried 3 times, then 10 s backoff. Wired in cmd/d2bot/d2dwire.go (ListRow tables). Drill after the fix: `aura up` once per area change or respawn, 0 failure lines.
- Ladder is data: internal/combat/classdata/profiles.json paladin: Prayer only while resting under 70 percent life, then Holy Fire when a crowd is near, then Might, then Thorns; band at clvl 18 with Blessed Hammer: Concentration first.
- Built-in weak points the owner may be seeing:
  1. Aura is only ever re-checked at KeepAura intervals; after a death or portal the first fights run without it (V keepAura order, U how long the gap is live).
  2. Merc aura is not part of the plan (no merc-aura code; V grep). 
  3. The older plan table build-paladin-normal30.json (Questy runs it) buys Resist Fire 2 and Holy Fire 4 before Zeal, while the profile's first ladder entry that is learned wins: Holy Fire only with a crowd, so most early fights run Might-less if Might was never bought (U: Questy legacy table row 2 buys Might 1, clvl 4-5 Resist Fire which the ladder never uses).

## 3. Is the skill plan sane for Normal 1-30?
- v2 plan (plandata/build-paladin-normal30-v2.json): Might 1 at clvl 2, Sacrifice 1 at clvl 3 (prereq of Zeal), Prayer 1 at 4, Might to 6-7 by clvl 11, Smite 1 at 10, Zeal 1 per level clvl 12-15 (4), Holy Fire clvl 16-19, Holy Bolt 20, Blessed Hammer from 21, Blessed Aim 22, Concentration 23, BH to clvl 30. Stats: 2 Str + 3 Vit per level early, then 1 Str + 2 Dex + 2 Vit. Sane for Zeal-then-Hammer (S).
- Sacrifice as a main skill at clvl 12 is NOT good (S): it costs life per swing (about 8 percent of current max life, U exact), has poor early damage, and the repo profile still lists it in the clvl 2 to 17 attack bands (profiles.json) while the sme doc says it was removed; the two disagree (U which profile the live bot loads). One point as Zeal prereq only is right. Questy at clvl 12 with Sacrifice 6 is the OLD table and is a waste of 5 points (S); the v2 table fixes it for fresh Paladins only.
- Human leveling Paladin for Normal, as the community plays it (S, Maxroll/Arreat style, not fetched this turn): points go Sacrifice 1 (clvl 6 in guides that open with Sacrifice), Zeal 1 at 12 then Zeal to 4-5 as soon as it unlocks, Might 1-5 early, Holy Fire from clvl 13-18 once Zeal is on, Charge/Smite only as prereqs; Hammerdin humans respec once into Blessed Hammer around clvl 24-30 (the free Akara respec comes from the Act 5 Akara quest reward, Normal has none before that; U). The repo explicitly chose no respec (docs/sme/class-paladin.md).
- Merc (S, V docs/sme/mercenary.md): Act 2 Desert Mercenary Defiance/Prayer in Normal, Offense Blessed Aim; Act 1 Rogue is the cheap early pick. Prayer on the merc heals the hero and merc and is separate from the hero aura.

## 4. Recommendations (data first)
1. Gate the early aura on actual learning (data): in the paladin profile start the ladder with Might for clvl 1-17 with no `Holy Fire` crowd rule until Holy Fire has 3 points; verify with the log line `aura up` and ActiveAura = 33 within 5 s of every area change.
2. Make Sacrifice a prereq-only skill (data, profile bands): remove it from the clvl 2-17 attack bands and from `lifePrice` so plain Attack with Might is used until Zeal; verify in a fight log that no life is lost per swing.
3. Use Prayer as a combat aura when hero life is under 50 percent AND potions are low (data: RestLifePct field), and put 3 points in the Vigor-free path; Prayer tick heals about the first 6 HP per second at level 3 (S, U). Verify life regen in a Cave 1 drill.
4. Re-apply the aura immediately on arriving in a new area and after respawn before the first monster in range (code-neutral change: KeepAura force=true on area change), and log the gap in ms; target under 1 s.
5. Plan the merc aura as data: hire the Act 2 Desert Mercenary Defiance in Normal (cost about 1,000 to 2,000 gold, U) once the hero reaches Lut Gholein and add a merc-aura read to the soak record; until then the Rogue.
Not a bug: the bot does not require the aura to stay selected, and reads state 350 from memory, so it is not "assuming it is on".
