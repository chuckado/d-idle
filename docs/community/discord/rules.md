# #rules (paste and pin)

1. Be kind. No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks. Disagree about ideas, not people.
2. No game files, CD keys, cracks, keygens or piracy help. Do not ask where to get Diablo II; do not share it. Bring your own copy.
3. This is an offline single-player tool. No discussion of botting, cheating or automating on Battle.net or realms, and no help with
   evading Blizzard's rules. Nothing here talks to Battle.net.
4. Tag claims: say whether something is verified (and how) or unverified. A guess is fine if called a guess.
5. No secrets: never post tokens, webhook URLs, passwords or private keys. Scrub paths, usernames and hostnames from logs first.
6. Stay on topic: d.idle, the bot, d2d as used by it, idle and bot-played Diablo II. No advertising, no unrelated self-promotion.
7. Use the channels and the pinned templates; one bug per thread; no cross-posting.
8. Be honest about AI: say when text or code you share was machine-written.
9. The moderator's word is final. Questions about a decision go to the moderator privately, not into the channel.
10. d.idle is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment. Diablo II is a trademark of Blizzard Entertainment.
