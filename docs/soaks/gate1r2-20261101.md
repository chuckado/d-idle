### 7. The HasTP root cause (V for the pattern, U for the fork side)
The `inv-tp-dump` lines explain it. Of 859 lines, 186 say `hastp:false`. 151 of them have a tome in `ContInventory` with `counted=0 raw70=no70` and 35 have no portal item at all (the character really has none: all 35 are in the first 7 minutes, before the first tome purchase). The 151 are the stat read: `PortalQty` returns 0 when the stat list has no stat 70 (internal/combat/portal.go:24, "an empty tome has no stat list"), and the d2d reader builds that list only when the dump's item quantity is above 0 (internal/d2denv/reader.go:139, `if ex.Qty > 0`). A tome whose dump row reads quantity 0 for a moment looks exactly like an empty tome. The pattern: the same tome reads 4, then no70 for 12 s, then 4 again (19:57:17 to 19:57:30), or 5 in the town and no70 in Cold Plains one second after the waypoint (20:24:28 true, 20:24:29 false, right after the shop bought 5 scrolls). 95 such cases are provably the same count before and after (V); 54 changed count between (a buy or pickup, a use) and 2 fell. `where=inventory` and the class were right every time: it is not a container or a class problem. Why the dump row carries quantity 0 (stale extras after an area change, or a refill in flight after a shop click) is the fork side (U: needs one `dump items` read in the first second after a waypoint with a tome at quantity above 0). Files and lines: internal/d2denv/reader.go:139, internal/combat/portal.go:24, consumers of the raw verdict internal/bot/resource.go:33 (kit gate kitgate.go:31, task.go:307 and 507, goal.go:217, 359, 364, broke.go:71 and 132, leveling.go:173, routeguard.go:86), the debounce internal/bot/tpdebounce.go (3 looks or 2 virtual minutes, about 10 s wall here: shorter than the 28 s drop-outs).
