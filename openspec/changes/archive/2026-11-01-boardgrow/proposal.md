## Why
The contributor board was sparse (6 open items) and finished items stayed in the open list. The user asked for more items and for done items to move to an archive.

## What Changes
- `docs/community/work-archive.md`: finished items (full brief kept, plus done date, who, result path); its table is generated by `tools/contrib/board.py --write`, `board.py --archive` moves every `status: done` section out of open-work.md. `board.py --check` fails while a done item is still in open-work.md.
- work-board.md lists only items that are not done, with a pointer to the archive.
- `chunker.py` sync and groom read open-work.md plus the archive (`board.load_all`): an archived item is never withdrawn or re-created, a still-open issue of an archived item is closed once.
- SKMELEE and SKRESIST moved to the archive; 12 new items (5 verify, 7 decomp) in open-work.md.
